About the Role
We are looking for a reliable and detail-oriented Geotechnical Technician to join our team, operating directly out of our customer’s onsite laboratory. In this hands-on role, you will perform routine sampling and laboratory testing of soils and aggregates to ensure civil and construction materials meet strict Australian Standards and project specifications.
Key Responsibilities
- Collect, prepare, and log soil and aggregate samples from active construction or quarry sites.
- Perform standard NATA-accredited laboratory and field tests (including moisture content, particle size distribution/sieve analysis, Atterberg limits, compaction/Proctor testing, CBR, and nuclear density gauge testing).
- Calibrate, clean, and maintain laboratory testing equipment to ensure accurate results.
- Accurately record test data, perform calculations, and prepare technical reports for site engineers and clients.
- Adhere strictly to site Work Health and Safety (WHS) policies, quality assurance systems, and Australian Standards (AS 1289 / AS 1141).
- Liaise professionally with site supervisors, engineers, and project managers regarding test results and scheduling.
About You
- Experience: Previous experience as a Construction Materials Testing (CMT) or Geotechnical Technician, specifically with soils and aggregates.
- Qualifications: Certificate III or IV in Laboratory Skills (or equivalent) is highly regarded, though experienced applicants without formal qualifications will be considered.
- Licences: Current manual driver’s licence (essential) and a White Card (Construction Induction). A Radiation Safety Licence (for nuclear density gauges) is a strong advantage.
- Skills: Strong attention to detail, solid basic maths skills, and familiarity with lab software or Microsoft Excel.
- Mindset: Able to work independently in an onsite environment, manage changing project timelines, and lift/handle heavy sample bags safely.
What’s on Offer
- Competitive hourly rate / salary package + overtime opportunities.
- Ongoing training and support to gain further NATA signatories or certifications.
- Opportunity to work closely with major infrastructure/construction projects.
- DIDO roster, with 7-days on/7-days off roster
