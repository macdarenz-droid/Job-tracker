Start your engineering career on real road and civil infrastructure projects.
Roadside Safety Solutions (RSS) is a growing Victorian road safety and infrastructure contractor delivering projects for councils, civil contractors and infrastructure clients across Victoria.
We’re looking for a Graduate Civil Engineer to join our team and develop a career in estimating, tendering and commercial project delivery.
No previous estimating experience is required. We’ll provide hands-on training and exposure to real projects across guardrail and safety barriers, linemarking, signage, road maintenance and civil infrastructure.
The role
Working with our estimating, operations and management teams, you’ll learn to:
- Review drawings, specifications, RFQs and tender documents
- Complete quantity take-offs
- Build labour, plant, material and subcontractor costs
- Obtain and compare supplier pricing
- Prepare quotations and schedules of rates
- Assist with council, government and major infrastructure tenders
- Identify scope requirements, risks and opportunities
- Visit project sites and learn how the work is delivered
- Assist with project handover from estimating to operations
About you
You’ll have recently completed, or be close to completing, a degree in Civil Engineering or a related engineering discipline.
We’re looking for someone who:
- Enjoys working with numbers and solving problems
- Has strong attention to detail
- Can interpret or is keen to learn engineering drawings
- Is confident using Excel and Microsoft Office
- Is organised, practical and keen to learn
- Has a genuine interest in civil construction and infrastructure
Previous industry, vacation or site experience is an advantage, but not essential.
Why join RSS?
- Graduate role with hands-on training
- Work on real Victorian road and infrastructure projects
- Gain both commercial and practical engineering experience
- Learn directly from experienced estimating and operations teams
- Develop skills across estimating, tendering and project delivery
- Career pathways into Estimating, Project Engineering, Project Management and Commercial Management
