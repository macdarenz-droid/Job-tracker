Why Join Georgiou - A STRABAG company
Join Georgiou and the CoastaltraX consortium for the Wave Stage 1 Greenfields package as a Site Engineer to join our civil and engineering teams. The jointly funded Queensland-Australian Government project, led by the Acciona-Georgiou CoastalTraX consortium, will deliver a brand-new dual-track rail corridor from Beerwah to Caloundra-cutting through greenfield land to connect growing communities with two new stations at Bells Creek (Aura) and Caloundra.

What you'll do (but not limited to): 
- Assist the Project Manager in fulfilling the contractual requirements and site targets across all project business functions
- Produce short term programs including resource levels for an assigned area of a project
- Assist in ensuring all equipment, materials and resources are available on or ahead of time to achieve the works program
- Participates in and actively contributes to toolbox meetings, pre-start meetings, site inductions, JHA's, Take 5's and work methods and practices and other forms of safety programs
- Assist in the allocation and processing of invoices and costs and produce productivity reports and analysis as required
What you're good at: 
- Demonstrated experience working as a Site Engineer in the civil construction industry 
- A working knowledge of implementing and monitoring project management plans
- Experience in coordinating and/or running toolbox meetings, pre-start meetings, site inductions and a demonstrated commitment to HSEQ practices
- Proven experience in managing a control system for labour, plant and materials
- Excellent interpersonal, written and verbal communication skills which enable you to assist in resolution of client and key stakeholders' concerns
- A Degree in Civil Engineering
