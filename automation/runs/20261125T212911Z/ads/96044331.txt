About Us
Morgan Consulting Engineers (MCE) is an established structural, civil and forensic engineering consultancy that has been operating in Brisbane since 1972.
With around 60 employees across multiple engineering disciplines, we're a friendly, down-to-earth team working across a diverse range of projects. We're looking for an experienced Civil Engineering Draftsperson to join our Civil team in a full-time, office-based position.
The Role
This is a hands-on civil design and drafting role where you'll work closely with our Civil Engineers to develop practical design solutions and produce accurate, coordinated project documentation.
You'll be involved in a variety of civil engineering projects, taking responsibility for allocated design and documentation tasks from initial project information through to engineering review.
Your responsibilities will include:
- Develop civil design solutions and prepare accurate drawings, models, plans, sections, details and associated project documentation.
- Use Civil 3D, PC Drain, PC Sewer, Bluebeam and other relevant civil design software.
- Interpret engineering requirements, survey information and project specifications to develop practical civil design solutions.
- Assist with civil design elements including levels, grading, drainage and associated infrastructure documentation.
- Work closely with Civil Engineers to coordinate design information, resolve technical queries and incorporate engineering feedback.
- Check calculations, models, drawings and documentation for accuracy, consistency and compliance with project requirements.
- Manage allocated tasks, priorities and deadlines across multiple projects.
- Identify and communicate design issues, missing information and potential project delivery risks early.
- Liaise with engineers, project team members and other stakeholders to support effective project delivery.
About You
We're looking for someone with a strong technical drafting background who enjoys problem-solving, takes ownership of their work and works well within a collaborative team.
Ideally, you'll have:
- A relevant qualification in Civil Engineering Drafting or a related discipline, and/or demonstrated relevant industry experience.
- Previous experience in civil design and drafting, preferably within an engineering consultancy.
- Demonstrated proficiency in Civil 3D and relevant civil design software.
- Experience with PC Drain, PC Sewer and Bluebeam, or the ability to develop capability in these systems.
- A sound understanding of civil design principles, levels, grading, drainage and engineering documentation.
- Strong attention to detail and a commitment to producing accurate, high-quality work.
- The ability to organise your workload, manage priorities and meet project deadlines.
- Good communication, problem-solving and teamwork skills.
