About Pioneer Water Tanks
Pioneer Water Tanks have been setting the industry standard in water storage for over 35 years, leading the way with advanced engineering and technology. We are a 100% Australian-owned and operated company, priding ourselves on maintaining strict control over our design and manufacturing processes to ensure every tank is built to the highest standards.
As a division of Ahrens, a fifth-generation Australian family business with a legacy spanning over 120 years, Pioneer is backed by the resources and expertise of a national leader in construction, engineering and rural infrastructure.
About the Role 
The Draftsperson at Pioneer Water Tanks, based in Bellevue, WA, is responsible for producing detailed technical drawings and plans in accordance with specifications from engineers, architects, and designers. This role requires precision, attention to detail, and proficiency in drafting software to support the design and construction of various projects.
Skills and Experience
- Relevant qualifications in drafting, engineering or a related field
- Strong hands-on experience with AutoCAD and Inventor
- Sound knowledge of technical drawing standards and principles
- Ability to read and interpret architectural and engineering drawings and requirements
- Detail-oriented, with a strong eye for accuracy
- Excellent communication and problem-solving skills
- Experience in construction, manufacturing or engineering is highly desirable
