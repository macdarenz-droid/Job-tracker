About WSce
WSce is an established multidisciplinary engineering consultancy providing civil, water and building services engineering solutions across Australia.
We are seeking a Civil Engineer to join our Civil & Water Infrastructure team, working on a range of water, wastewater and stormwater infrastructure projects.
About the Role
Working with senior engineers, your responsibilities will include:
- Design and documentation of water, sewer and stormwater infrastructure.
- Hydraulic analysis, engineering calculations and infrastructure planning.
- Preparation of engineering drawings using AutoCAD and Civil 3D.
- Application of WSA codes, Australian Standards and relevant authority specifications.
- Preparation and coordination of design submissions for authority approvals.
- Liaison with clients, consultants and relevant authorities.
- Site inspections and technical support.
- Delivery of accurate engineering documentation within project timelines.
About You
To be successful, you will have:
- A degree in Civil Engineering.
- Minimum 12 months’ relevant local experience in civil or water infrastructure engineering within a consulting environment.
- Intermediate to advanced AutoCAD skills.
- Experience with 3D terrain modelling software, preferably Civil 3D.
- Knowledge of WSA codes and water authority requirements.
- Ability to meet Sydney Water's C1 Designer competency requirements.
- Strong technical, communication and problem-solving skills.
- Ability to work independently on assigned tasks under senior engineering supervision.
What We Offer
- Exposure to diverse civil and water infrastructure projects.
- Supportive and collaborative engineering team.
- Professional development and career progression opportunities.
- Competitive remuneration based on experience.
