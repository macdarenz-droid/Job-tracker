About the role
This company is a leading contractor delivering high-profile infrastructure and civil engineering excellence across Australia. Known for innovation, structural integrity, and high safety standards, we pride ourselves on building communities and delivering complex projects on time and within budget. We are seeking an ambitious, analytical, and highly organized Site Engineer to join our project delivery team for a major flagship infrastructure project in the heart of Brisbane CBD. Reporting directly to the Project Manager , you will bridge the gap between design plans and practical onsite execution. You will play an active role in daily operations, ensuring that civil and structural works meet rigid quality standards, safety protocols, and strict timelines.
Key responsibilities
- Site Supervision & Execution
- Oversee and monitor daily on-site construction activities to ensure they align directly with blueprints, technical specifications, and design drawings.
- Directly supervise subcontractors, plant operators, and site crews, tracking their progress against weekly targets.
- Provide clear technical guidance and problem-solving solutions to resolve construction-related bottlenecks efficiently.
- Planning, Logistics & Spoil Management
- Manage short-term planning schedules, including 4-week lookahead schedules and daily construction sequencing.
- Implement and manage the site spoil disposal tracking system, ensuring precise monitoring of excavated material volumes, truck movements, and load counts.
- Coordinate the procurement, tracking, and timely delivery and exporting of construction materials (e.g., spoil, concrete and steel components) while minimizing waste.
- Conduct preliminary site inspections and work alongside surveyors to verify earthworks, leveling, and site boundaries.
3. Quality Assurance & Compliance
- Implement and manage Inspection and Test Plans (ITPs) and prepare Inspection and Test Reports (ITRs) to secure sign-off from clients and QA/QC teams.
- Ensure absolute compliance with Queensland environmental and waste regulations by retaining accurate waste transport certificates, dockets, and spoil classification records.
- Draft and manage Requests for Information (RFIs) to resolve design discrepancies with architects and consultants.
- Maintain accurate project documentation, including daily site diaries, tracking logs, and variation records.
4. Safety & Commercial Management
- Enforce absolute compliance with Australian Work Health and Safety (WHS) regulations, ensuring all personnel wear mandated PPE and adhere to Safe Work Method Statements (SWMS).
- Assist the Project Manager with cost tracking, financial reporting, and reviewing quotes to prevent budget overruns.
