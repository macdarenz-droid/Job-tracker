Bielby is experiencing a period of sustained growth, supported by a strong pipeline of major infrastructure opportunities and continued expansion into new regions. Building on over 40 years of successful major project delivery, we are seeking Civil Engineering Graduates to join our team in NSW and be part of the first major infrastructure project win in NSW. Bielby has been awarded the Kempsey-Armidale Road Restoration Project (K2A) by Armidale Regional Council, marking our first major award since establishing a permanent NSW presence in early 2026. It also represents our largest infrastructure restoration and recovery contract to date, a key pillar of Bielby’s growth and diversification strategy.
The project will restore 43 kilometres of Kempsey-Armidale Road and a five-kilometre section of Lower Creek Road following damage caused by the 2019 bushfires and subsequent flood events in 2020 and 2022. You will be part of a major regional infrastructure project that will restore and strengthen critical transport links across regional New South Wales. More importantly, it is an opportunity to play a key role in Bielby's strategic expansion within NSW.
Reporting to a Project Engineer or Senior Project Engineer, you will gain valuable and practical on the ground experience. On a 2-year Graduate program, you will support construction and delivery but also get exposure to quality, commercial, safety and environment.
What You'll Do (with guidance and training):
- Assist with project planning, organisation, and programming
- Support in the procurement of materials and services
- Assist in conducting safety inspections and maintaining compliance
- Perform quality assurance checks and site inspections
- Review drawings and issuing RFIs
- Assist with site survey 
What You'll Bring
- Ability to work independently and within a team environment
- Excellent written and written communication skills
- Safety focused mindset with a solid work ethic
- Willingness to learn and be a valued member of the team
- Current Australian Driver’s license
- Bachelor’s degree in civil engineering obtained in the last 12 months.
These roles will be drive-in drive out on a 10 days on, 4 days off roster with camp accommodation and food provided unless you live local to the project.
