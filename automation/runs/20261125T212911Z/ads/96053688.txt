Who we are and why you want to work for us
GreenTec Group is a specialist engineering consulting firm based in Brisbane. We are looking to expand our Brisbane team and are currently seeking a full-time Civil Engineer to contribute to our growing portfolio of design and construction projects across Australia.
GreenTec specialises in waste management, landfill engineering, environmental solutions, and waste and water containment. With an established reputation for delivering practical, high-quality engineering solutions, we work with long-standing clients across both the private and public sectors.
We aren't your typical consultancy. While we deliver the same high-quality outcomes as larger consulting firms, our focus is on providing practical advice and innovative solutions, supporting our clients through all stages of their projects, from initial approvals and design through to construction, operations, closure and rehabilitation.
As a boutique consulting firm, we offer employees the opportunity to work across a diverse range of projects, with competitive remuneration, flexible working arrangements, specialist training and opportunities for career progression.
About the role
As a Civil Engineer, you will contribute to the design, planning and delivery of civil engineering projects, particularly within the landfill, waste management, mining and environmental sectors. Working as part of a collaborative team, your responsibilities will include:
- Undertaking civil engineering design, modelling and technical analysis for landfill, stormwater, mining and large earthworks projects.
- Interpreting laboratory and field data, preparing technical reports and providing engineering recommendations.
- Preparing and reviewing engineering drawings, specifications and project deliverables.
- Providing practical engineering advice and technical support throughout project design and construction.
- Assisting with the preparation of project proposals, budgets and tender documentation.
- Coordinating engineering projects, contractors and project resources to ensure timely and efficient delivery.
- Undertaking site inspections, fieldwork and construction quality assurance (CQA) activities.
- Liaising with clients, contractors and other project stakeholders throughout the project lifecycle.
- Ensuring engineering activities are undertaken in accordance with relevant Australian Standards, project specifications and safety requirements.
Minimum requirements
- A recognised degree in Civil Engineering or a closely related engineering discipline.
- At least two years of relevant professional experience in civil engineering design, construction or project delivery.
·         Experience in landfill engineering, waste management or large earthworks projects would be advantageous. RPEQ registration, or eligibility to obtain registration, would also be highly regarded.
- Knowledge of relevant Australian engineering standards and construction practices.
- Strong technical, analytical and problem-solving abilities.
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
- Strong attention to detail and the ability to work efficiently in a fast-paced environment.
- Ability to work effectively both independently and as part of a team.
- A positive, proactive attitude and willingness to contribute to a collaborative working environment.
- A current driver's licence and willingness to travel to project sites as required.
