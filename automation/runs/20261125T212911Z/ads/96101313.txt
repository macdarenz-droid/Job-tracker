The Role:
As Site Engineer, you will provide technical and operational support across all phases of project delivery, ensuring works are planned, coordinated and executed in line with programme, budget, quality and safety objectives.
You will work closely with site teams, subcontractors, suppliers, consultants and clients while gaining exposure to all aspects of civil project delivery.
Your responsibilities will include:
- Providing day-to-day engineering support across active construction projects
- Coordinating site activities to ensure works are delivered safely, efficiently and in accordance with approved methodologies
- Assisting with project planning, programming and short-term works planning
- Reviewing construction drawings, specifications and contract documentation
- Preparing and managing Inspection and Test Plans (ITPs), lot packages and quality documentation
- Undertaking site inspections and ensuring compliance with project specifications and standards
- Coordinating survey requirements including set out, conformance and as-constructed information
- Managing subcontractors and suppliers to ensure project deliverables are achieved
- Assisting with procurement activities and material management
- Monitoring project progress and supporting programme updates
- Preparing daily, weekly and monthly project reports
- Tracking quantities, productivity, costs and project performance metrics
- Supporting the management of project variations and change control processes
- Assisting with project forecasting, cost tracking and budget management
- Ensuring compliance with quality, environmental and safety requirements
- Identifying and resolving technical issues as they arise during construction
- Liaising with clients, consultants, local authorities and key project stakeholders
- Supporting project handover and close-out activities
