The Company
Wagstaff Piling is a privately owned Australian foundation engineering company servicing clients throughout Australia and New Zealand. For over 40 years, we have provided specialist design and construction services for piles and related techniques such as diaphragm walls and ground improvement.
Our professional team has successfully completed more than 6,000 projects, encompassing all types of foundations in a range of ground conditions for civil engineering and building applications. Our reputation is built on developing technically excellent staff at all levels and utilising the most advanced foundation engineering equipment in the industry.
The Opportunity
We have an exciting opportunity for a Civil Engineer to join our team in Melbourne, VIC on a permanent, full-time basis. Reporting to the State Construction Manager, you will plan, design, organise and oversee the construction of civil engineering projects, managing piling and foundation works from contract award through to completion, with input into tender and design for a variety of civil and building projects.
Key Responsibilities:
- Plan, organise and oversee the construction of piling and foundation projects, coordinating site activities, labour, materials, plant and equipment from mobilisation through completion.
- Review designs, specifications, drawings and geotechnical information, and determine construction methods, materials and quality standards.
- Analyse soil, rock and structural conditions to support safe, practical foundation design and construction solutions.
- Contribute to tenders and cost estimates, and monitor project budgets and programs.
- Manage contract obligations, including design changes, variations and extensions of time.
- Provide clear direction to supervisors, crews and subcontractors, and mentor team members.
- Act as a point of contact for clients and maintain strong commercial relationships.
- Maintain project documentation, reporting and quality records, and ensure compliance with our Integrated Management System, legislation and client requirements.
- Promote a safety-first culture and continuous improvement, and progress towards Chartered Professional Engineer (CPEng) status.
About You:
- Bachelor’s degree or higher in Civil Engineering (or equivalent) is required.
- Membership of Engineers Australia (Chartered status preferred); relevant registration (e.g Registered Building Practitioner) is desirable.
- Minimum 3 years’ relevant work experience in geotechnical/foundation, building, or civil engineering is essential.
- Solid understanding of geotechnical and structural engineering principles.
- Experience in project management, design, cost estimating and tendering.
- Proficiency in project scheduling, AutoCAD and Microsoft Office tools.
- Strong communication, leadership and organisational skills.
- Commitment to safety, quality and innovation, with the ability to work independently and as part of a team.
- Ability to work away around the country on projects as required
