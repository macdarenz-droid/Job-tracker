The Company
COLAS Australia, a leader in the Road Construction and Maintenance Industry, continues to expand its operations throughout Australia and New Zealand as a company which is now supported locally by over 700 employees, across 15 work sites in 5 states. Colas is a subsidiary of the Bouygues Group which includes over 200,000 staff worldwide.
The Role
We’re looking for a Laboratory Technician to join our team based in Caloundra, QLD. You’ll play an important role in delivering accurate and reliable laboratory and field testing, supporting the quality and performance of our road construction and surfacing operations.
Key responsibilities include:
- Perform accurate and timely laboratory and field testing.
- Ensure testing meets ISO 17025, NATA and internal quality requirements.
- Maintain accurate test results, reports and laboratory records.
- Operate and maintain laboratory equipment safely and effectively.
- Follow safety requirements, including the use and transport of radiation gauges.
- Identify and report safety, quality and testing non-conformances.
- Support continuous improvement across laboratory operations and testing processes.
- Liaise with internal teams, clients, suppliers and industry stakeholders.
- Maintain independence and impartiality when conducting testing.
The Ideal Candidate will possess the following essential skills and experience:
- Preferably a Cert 3 or 4 in Laboratory Techniques
- Proven success in a technical or laboratory environment
- Valid Driver’s license 
- Strong mathematical or technical abilities
- Ability to work flexible hours including night shift & weekends as required
- Flexibility to travel and work away when required (LAHA Allowance)
- Ability to work under pressure and work through issues
- Self-motivated with the ability to follow instructions
