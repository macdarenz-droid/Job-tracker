Western Geotechnical & Laboratory Services
Vasse, South West WA | Immediate Start | Full-time following Christmas break
About Us
Western Geotechnical & Laboratory Services is a Western Australian-owned and operated business providing construction materials testing, geotechnical and specialist laboratory services across metropolitan and regional Western Australia.
With the continued growth of our South West operations, we are seeking an enthusiastic, reliable and motivated individual to join our Vasse laboratory as a Trainee Laboratory Technician.
About the Role
This is an entry-level, hands-on position offering the opportunity to develop a career within the construction materials testing, civil engineering and mining industries.
The role involves a combination of laboratory-based testing and outdoor fieldwork, supporting a range of residential, commercial, civil infrastructure and mining projects.
No previous laboratory experience is required, with comprehensive on-the-job training provided by our experienced technicians and management team.
We are looking for someone available for an immediate start, with some initial training required at our Welshpool laboratory in Perth to develop the necessary foundation of skills and knowledge.
Working hours over the Christmas and New Year period may be limited due to project and operational requirements. These arrangements, along with the initial Perth training requirements, will be discussed further with the successful applicant.
Following the Christmas break, the position will transition to full-time employment based at our Vasse laboratory, with potential FIFO opportunities available as the successful applicant develops their skills and experience.
Key Responsibilities
- Undertaking laboratory testing of soils, aggregates and concrete.
- Assisting with field testing on construction and civil engineering projects.
- Sample preparation, handling and registration.
- Operation of laboratory and field testing equipment.
- Recording test results and maintaining accurate technical records.
- General laboratory and equipment maintenance.
- Assisting senior technicians with day-to-day operational requirements.
- Working in accordance with Australian Standards, company procedures and quality assurance requirements.
- Maintaining a safe, clean and organised working environment.
About You
We are looking for someone with a positive attitude, a willingness to learn and a genuine interest in developing practical and technical skills.
This is a physically active role that involves working with equipment, handling construction materials and spending time outdoors in varying weather conditions.
We value reliability, initiative, attention to detail and a strong work ethic. Previous industry experience is not essential, but a willingness to listen, learn and follow established processes is fundamental to succeeding in this position.
Skills and Requirements
- Current driver's licence, preferably manual.
- Reliable transport to and from our Vasse laboratory.
- Good basic mathematics, literacy and computer skills.
- Ability to undertake physically demanding work.
- Strong attention to detail and willingness to follow procedures.
- Ability to work both independently and as part of a team.
- Willingness to undertake initial training in Perth.
- Availability for an immediate start.
- Interest in potential future FIFO opportunities.
A Construction White Card would be advantageous but is not essential.
School leavers and individuals looking for a career change are encouraged to apply.
What We Offer
- Immediate commencement with structured on-the-job training.
- Full-time employment following the Christmas and New Year break.
- A locally based position within the South West.
- A combination of laboratory and outdoor fieldwork.
- Opportunities to obtain nationally recognised laboratory qualifications.
- Career progression into experienced and senior technician positions.

- Exposure to a diverse range of construction, civil engineering and mining projects.
- Long-term career development within an established Western Australian business.
