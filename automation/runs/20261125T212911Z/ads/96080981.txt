About the role
As a Junior Design Visualisation Specialist, you will work with engineers, designers and project teams to turn engineering designs and technical information into clear, accurate and engaging visual outputs for design reviews, stakeholder engagement, tenders, construction planning and Safety in Design.
You will prepare still images, animations, fly-throughs and interactive visualisations using Twinmotion, Unreal Engine or similar real-time tools. As visualisation demand develops, you will also build capability in mechanical drafting using AutoCAD and Plant 3D, providing useful project support while developing a broader understanding of water and wastewater infrastructure.
The role is suited to someone from game design, animation, architectural visualisation, digital media or a related 3D field who wants to apply strong modelling, spatial reasoning and real-time rendering skills to engineering projects. You will receive structured drafting development, mentoring from experienced designers and engineers, and opportunities to contribute to digital automation and internal tools.
Key Responsibilities
- Produce clear, accurate and engaging visualisations of water, wastewater and other infrastructure projects using Twinmotion, Unreal Engine or similar real-time software.
- Create still images, animations, camera fly-throughs, construction staging sequences and interactive presentations suited to design reviews, stakeholder engagement, tenders and Safety in Design.
- Import, clean and prepare models from Civil 3D, Plant 3D, Revit, Navisworks and other common design formats for real-time visualisation.
- Work with project teams to understand the intended audience, key messages and level of visual detail required.
- Present complex engineering designs in a form that clients, operators, community stakeholders and non-technical audiences can readily understand.
- Maintain an organised and repeatable workflow for source models, textures, linked files, visualisation scenes and published outputs.
- Develop efficient templates, asset libraries and reusable workflows that improve the quality and consistency of CMP’s visualisation work.
- Develop capability in mechanical drafting and modelling using AutoCAD, Plant 3D and CMP drafting standards under the guidance of an experienced mechanical designer and mechanical engineer.
- Prepare and update mechanical drawings, models and associated documentation as assigned, with all drafting outputs checked through CMP review processes.
- Contribute to automation and digital improvement work where your technical skills are relevant, including CAD and Plant 3D automation, model-to-Twinmotion workflows and simple internal tools.
Professional Skills
- Demonstrated experience using Twinmotion, Unreal Engine or comparable real-time visualisation software.
- Experience producing high-quality still images, animations, fly-throughs or interactive 3D environments.
- A sound understanding of composition, lighting, materials, textures, camera movement and visual storytelling.
- Experience importing, cleaning, optimising and preparing 3D geometry from CAD, BIM or general-purpose modelling software.
- A portfolio demonstrating relevant 3D visualisation work, together with strong communication, file-management and problem-solving skills.
Qualifications
- A qualification in 3D design, animation, architectural visualisation, digital media, game design, visual effects, drafting, engineering design or a related discipline.
