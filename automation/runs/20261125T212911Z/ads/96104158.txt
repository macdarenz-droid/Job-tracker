About the role
Join a laboratory team responsible for analysing asbestos samples using Polarised Light Microscopy (PLM) and Transmission Electron Microscopy (TEM). The role supports accurate asbestos identification and reliable laboratory outcomes by following established quality management procedures, analytical methods and quality system requirements.
Key responsibilities
- Carry out laboratory duties associated with asbestos sample analysis.
- Analyse asbestos samples using PLM and TEM techniques.
- Identify asbestos types in material samples accurately and consistently.
- Follow documented quality management procedures, analytical methods and quality system requirements.
- Maintain accurate laboratory records and support the delivery of reliable, compliant results.
What we're looking for
- A degree in Science, Building Engineering, Occupational Health and Safety or an allied field is desirable.
- Experience analysing asbestos samples using PLM and TEM.
- Endorsement as an analyst and signatory for the identification of asbestos types in material samples is advantageous.
- Experience working within a NATA-accredited laboratory environment is advantageous.
- Strong attention to detail and a commitment to quality and procedural compliance.
