Project Engineer - Brisbane Main Roads Project
Following the award of a major road infrastructure project in Brisbane, we are expanding our delivery team and looking for an experienced Project Engineer to take ownership of key elements of the works as the project moves into mobilisation and delivery.
This is an opportunity to join the project from the outset, work closely with an experienced project leadership team and play a hands-on role in delivering significant civil infrastructure for Brisbane.
What you’ll do
- Taking end-to-end ownership of assigned work areas, from planning and procurement through to construction and completion.
- Driving programme, productivity, quality and cost outcomes 
- Coordinating subcontractors, suppliers and site teams 
- Preparing and managing ITPs, RFIs, technical documentation and project records 
- Monitoring progress against programme and identifying delivery risks early 
- Supporting procurement, forecasting, quantities and cost control 
- Resolving technical and construction issues in collaboration with the project team 
- Building effective relationships with clients, designers and project stakeholders 
- Ensuring works are delivered safely and in accordance with project specifications 
What you’ll bring
- A degree in Civil Engineering or an equivalent qualification 
- Previous Project Engineer experience with a civil contractor on TMR projects
- Demonstrated experience across roads, bulk earthworks, drainage, pavements, structures or related civil infrastructure 
- Strong planning, coordination and problem-solving capability 
- Commercial awareness, including subcontractor management, forecasting and cost control 
- Confidence working with clients, designers, subcontractors and site teams 
- A strong commitment to safe, high-quality project delivery
