Remo Contractors Pty Ltd are offering an exciting full-time employment contract opportunity for a Junior/Graduate Civil Construction Project Engineer to join our family owned and operated business.
As our business opportunities expand, we are looking to add a highly motivated, results driven individual to our team.
Our Business
Remo Contractors Pty Ltd is a family owned business that provides civil construction, concrete and transport services to Local Government Councils, tier one firms and builders throughout South Australia. Our scope of works allows our employees to develop a broad range of skillsets whilst achieving job satisfaction in a rewarding work environment.
About the role
Your responsibilities will include but are not limited to:
- Define project objectives, scope, roles and responsibilities
- Work closely with the Senior Project Management Team
- Outline budgets based on requirements and tracking costs to deliver project on budget
- Ensure all work is completed in line with the company's quality management system
- Manage the relationship and communication with the client and all stakeholders, ensuring works are delivered to their satisfaction
- Management of sub-contractors, including performance monitoring and financial management
- Drafting and submitting progress claims and invoices
- Undertake frequent site inspections and audits to assess compliance to HSEQ policies and procedures
