The Role:
Swan Materials, part of CRH, is seeking a full-time Technician – Materials to join our Quality & Technical team. This is primarily a quarry-based role at The Lakes, WA, with a planned transition to our Hazelmere laboratory from February 2027.
Your main focus will be sampling, preparing and testing quarry materials, including aggregates and soils, through routine field sampling and laboratory testing. The role also offers the opportunity to gain valuable hands-on experience in concrete and asphalt sampling and testing, providing exposure to different areas of materials testing across the industry.
We welcome candidates with experience in any of these areas who are keen to broaden their technical skills. You don't need to know everything from day one – we are happy to provide training and support to help you develop your knowledge and experience.
Working alongside an experienced and supportive Quality & Technical team, you will help ensure our products meet relevant Australian Standards and NATA requirements, while developing your skills in a safety-focused environment.
Salary: $35 – $45 per hour, plus superannuation, depending on experience and qualifications.
Key Responsibilities:
- Assist with the preparation, processing and testing of aggregate and soil samples, as well as concrete and asphalt testing as required.
- Carry out routine field sampling and quality testing at the quarry.
- Assist with trial concrete mixes and the calibration and maintenance of laboratory equipment.
- Maintain laboratory equipment and consumables.
- Undertake quality assurance and quality control testing in accordance with relevant Australian Standards and Inspection and Test Plans (ITPs).
- Monitor, record and report on product quality and compliance.
- Collect QA and commercial samples safely and within required timeframes.
- Maintain accurate laboratory records, worksheets, certificates and reports in accordance with NATA requirements.
- Support internal and external customers through regular communication and updates.
- Assist with HSE inspections and comply with all company safety policies and procedures.
- Enter and maintain testing data in the Laboratory Management System.
What you will need to succeed:
- Effective communication skills (written & verbal)
- Meticulous analytical skills
- Ability to work autonomously but to also contribute collaboratively in a strong team environment;
- Strong literacy, numeracy and computer skills
- High level of data entry accuracy and record keeping with strong attention to detail
- Ability to lift 20kg samples
- Experience working in a fast-paced environment and ability to manage multiple priorities to meet deadlines
- Commitment to Health, Safety, Environment, Quality, and Wellbeing
- Minimum of Certificate III in Laboratory Skills (or equivalent), Current WA Drivers Licence, White Card
Desirable:
- Experience in Construction Materials testing and sampling
- Demonstrated experiences working with, Australian Standards, NATA ISO 17025 Certificate, Quality Assurance Standards
- Proficient in Microsoft Suite and Laboratory Management software (Desirable)
About Swan Materials:
Now part of Adbri, Swan Materials has been trusted by Western Australians, from its origins on the Swan River to its home today at Cockburn Cement’s Kwinana facility. It has helped build the foundations of our homes, roads, and communities. Now, the Swan name has evolved beyond cement as we grow our presence in Western Australia with the addition of select BGCs concrete, quarries, transport, asphalt, and materials technology centre. We are proud to introduce and unite these businesses as Swan Materials, a new name for a trusted partner.
About Adbri:
Adbri is majority owned by CRH and is a market leader in the manufacture and supply of construction materials and industrial minerals which has been building a better Australia since 1882. Adbri's respected brands employ over 2800 people nationally producing cement, lime, concrete, aggregates, concrete products and industrial minerals. 
About CRH:
CRH (NYSE: CRH) is the leading global provider of building materials solutions that build, connect and improve our world. Employing 80,000 people at 4,000 operating locations in 28 countries, CRH has market leadership positions in North America, Europe and Australia. As the essential partner for transportation and critical infrastructure projects, complex non-residential construction and outdoor living solutions, CRH’s unique offering of materials, products and value-added services helps to deliver a more resilient and sustainable built environment. The company is ranked among sector leaders by Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) rating agencies. A Fortune Global 500 company, CRH’s shares are listed on the NYSE and LSE.
