Sabre Engineering Services is seeking a Civil Project Engineer for a site based roadworks contract in Mackay. Civil construction delivery experience on Queensland Department of Transport and Main Roads (TMR) projects is essential. Our client is looking for someone to start as soon as available. The initial work package is on a 5/2 night shift roster, Sunday to Thursday.
 About the role
 As the Project Systems Representative, you’ll work with the site team to coordinate construction, maintain quality records and keep the programme and progress reporting up to date.
 - Plan and coordinate civil work packages with the site team
 - Track progress against the construction programme and prepare project reports
 - Coordinate inspections and maintain quality records in line with TMR specifications
 - Support commercial administration and keep records of changes to the works
 What you’ll bring
 - Experience as a Project Engineer delivering civil construction work on TMR road projects
 - Practical experience applying TMR specifications and managing construction quality records
 - Experience coordinating site works, updating programmes and reporting progress
 - Availability to work on site in Mackay and cover the initial Sunday to Thursday night shift roster
 How to apply
 Apply through SEEK with your CV. Include the TMR projects you’ve worked on, the work packages you looked after, your earliest availability and whether you can work the initial night shift roster.
