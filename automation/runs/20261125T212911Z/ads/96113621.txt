The Role
Riddwood is seeking a Digital Engineering Draftsperson to join our team and contribute to major infrastructure projects shaping the nation’s future.
In this role, you will work on complex projects such as tunnels, bridges, and large-scale civil structures. You’ll play a key role in developing and managing BIM models and construction documentation, delivering digital solutions that integrate structural engineering with construction methodology.
Working closely with a team of experienced engineers who understand both design and build, you’ll have the opportunity to apply your digital expertise to solve real construction challenges and see your contributions come to life on site.
If you’re passionate about digital design and engineering, enjoy combining technology with practical problem-solving, and want to be part of a collaborative and innovative team, this is the role for you.
Key Responsibilities
- Preparing detailed general arrangement, temporary works and construction drawings for tunnels, bridges and other civil structures, in support of Riddwood’s engineers.
- Managing own drafting tasks independently and applying technical judgement to resolve typical documentation issues.
- Producing accurate and efficient 2D drawings and 3D models in AutoCAD and Revit, coordinating directly with engineers and clients.
- Maintaining model and drawing accuracy across project phases, ensuring compliance with Riddwood’s standards and client requirements.
- Preparing and updating documentation including drawing registers and mark-ups.
- Supporting digital project delivery through consistent communication, attention to detail, and proactive coordination.
- Taking full ownership of the drafting task's delivery, ensuring accuracy, quality, and timely completion across multiple projects.
About You
You are a person who thrives in a fast paced environment. With 3+ years’ minimum experience from a consulting or construction background undertaking structural documentation, you should also have:
- Proficiency in AutoCAD and Revit, with experience in 3DS Max or Synchro 4D highly regarded.
- Strong attention to detail and commitment to learn Riddwood’s standards and workflows.
- Ability to manage multiple drafting tasks independently while maintaining accuracy and quality.
- Strong communication skills and ability to work collaboratively with engineers and clients.
- Thorough understanding of drawing standards, documentation workflows, and model coordination.
- Diploma or higher in Civil/Structural Drafting or a related discipline is advantageous.
What We Offer
- A generous salary package, and all the necessary bells & whistles to perform your job well.
- Become part of one of the largest Temporary Works specialist teams in Australia.
