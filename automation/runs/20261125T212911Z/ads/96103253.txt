About the business
 Join one of Victoria’s most prominent civil contractors. For over 25 years, Rokon has partnered with some of the state’s largest developers and infrastructure owners, delivering projects on time and on budget.
 We’re growing a diverse, forward-thinking team and we’re looking for skilled, driven Project and Site Engineers to work on projects across Melbourne. We are actively seeking candidates for a January 2027 start with interviews to be conducted before Christmas.
 You’ll be part of our Engineering team, providing the engineering and project management expertise behind the planning, budgeting, forecasting and reporting of our projects.
 About the opportunity
 Reporting to the Project Manager, your responsibilities will include, but are not limited to:
 - Deliver civil infrastructure projects safely, on time and within budget.
 - Manage project documentation, quality, HSE requirements, contracts and technical specifications.
 - Coordinate subcontractors, councils, authorities and internal stakeholders to keep projects moving.
 - Monitor costs, forecasts and project performance, proactively identifying and resolving issues.
 - Lead project meetings, provide regular reporting and drive successful project outcomes.
 About you
 To be considered, you’ll need to demonstrate:
 - Degree-qualified Civil Engineer with 2+ years’ experience in civil construction, ideally across subdivisions and land development.
 - Strong understanding of technical drawings, specifications, project planning and cost management.
 - Proven ability to build relationships and collaborate with clients, subcontractors, authorities and internal teams.
 - Highly organised with strong problem-solving, communication and stakeholder management skills.
 - Current White Card and Victorian Driver’s Licence, with a proactive, can-do approach to project delivery.
