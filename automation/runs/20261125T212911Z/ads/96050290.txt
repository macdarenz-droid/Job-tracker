About the Role 
Reporting to the Executive Manager of Operations, you’ll work closely with Sales, Projects, Manufacturing and Purchasing teams, preparing detailed drawings, 3D models, manufacturing documentation and CNC cutting files to support projects from concept through to production.
 No two projects are necessarily the same, offering variety, technical challenges and the opportunity to contribute to the development of customised solutions.
Key responsibilities include:
 - Preparing sales and project drawings for client review and approval
 - Developing detailed fabrication, assembly and component drawings
 - Creating Bills of Materials (BOMs) and CNC cutting files
 - Supporting product design and development, ensuring solutions are practical and manufacturable
 - Assisting with project engineering activities, including site measurements, layouts and take-offs
 - Collaborating with internal teams to troubleshoot and resolve design and drafting challenges
 - Managing drawing revisions and maintaining accurate project documentation
 - Ensuring drafting work is completed accurately and within project deadlines
