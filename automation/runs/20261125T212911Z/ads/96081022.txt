Kokatha Mining Services (KMS) is seeking four reliable and motivated Civil Technicians to support its contracted operations at BHP's Olympic Dam site in South Australia.
These positions provide an opportunity to work within a major mining operation, undertaking routine inspections, minor maintenance, housekeeping and reporting across external infrastructure and non-controlled site areas.
With four positions available and immediate start opportunities from the client's perspective, we encourage interested candidates to apply.
About the role
Working across BHP's Olympic Dam operation, Civil Technicians will be responsible for routine inspections, identifying maintenance requirements, completing minor maintenance activities and maintaining clean, safe and functional external areas.
Key responsibilities will include:
1. Carpark and shelter maintenance
- Inspect external carparks, parking areas and shelters.
- Identify and report defects or maintenance requirements.
- Undertake minor repairs and general housekeeping.
- Empty bins and collect rubbish as required.
2. Roads and signage
- Inspect road signage across designated site and industrial areas.
- Identify damaged, missing or deteriorating signage.
- Check warning, information, directional and speed signage.
- Undertake minor maintenance and housekeeping.
3. Footpaths and pedestrian areas
- Inspect pedestrian crossings, footpaths and roadside safety fencing.
- Identify hazards, damage and maintenance requirements.
- Complete minor maintenance and housekeeping.
4. Gardens and external areas
- Inspect office gardens and landscaped areas.
- Identify fallen trees, unsafe vegetation and maintenance requirements.
- Undertake general grounds maintenance and housekeeping.
5. Stormwater drainage
- Inspect stormwater infrastructure, ponds, drains, pits and easements.
- Identify blockages, damage and maintenance requirements.
- Undertake minor maintenance and housekeeping.
6. Fencing and site access gates
- Inspect lease boundary fencing and designated access gates.
- Identify damage, defects and security concerns.
- Undertake minor maintenance where appropriate.
7. Lighting and roads
- Inspect external building and roadside lighting.
- Inspect designated access roads, including powerline access roads, Borefield Road and industrial areas.
- Identify and report defects, damage and maintenance requirements.
- Undertake minor maintenance and housekeeping within the scope of the work.
About you
We are looking for candidates who demonstrate:
- Experience in civil maintenance, general maintenance, grounds maintenance, facilities maintenance or similar hands-on work.
- Practical skills and confidence using basic maintenance tools and equipment.
- Ability to identify hazards, defects and maintenance requirements.
- Good observation and reporting skills.
- A strong commitment to workplace health and safety.
- Ability to work independently and as part of a team.
- Reliability, initiative and a positive work ethic.
- Ability to follow site procedures and work instructions.
Previous mining or industrial site experience will be highly regarded.
Relevant licences, tickets, qualifications and competencies will be considered as part of the selection process.
Safety and site requirements
Successful candidates must be prepared to:
- Comply with BHP site safety standards and contractor requirements.
- Participate in required site inductions and training.
- Wear prescribed personal protective equipment.
- Follow task-specific risk assessments and safe work procedures.
- Report incidents, hazards and maintenance issues in accordance with site requirements.
