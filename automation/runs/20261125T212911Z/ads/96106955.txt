About the role
The Graduate
Civil Engineer will contribute to engineering investigations and project
delivery activities, including data collection and technical analysis. You will
assist in the preparation of technical documentation, designs and
specifications in accordance with Council standards.
The ideal
candidate will be enthusiastic, proactive, driven and innovative, and be able
to work collaboratively across teams within the service unit and broader
organisation. You will be involved in a variety of projects and be able to work
effectively independently and in teams.
Skills and experience
- Completion of a tertiary degree in civil
 engineering or a related discipline within the last 2 years.
- Well-developed written and verbal communication
skills, with a high level of attention to detail, accuracy and professionalism.
- Sound organisational skills, with the ability to
plan, prioritise and manage your work to meet objectives and deadlines.
- A strong collaborator who adds value to the
 organisation with proven ability to maintain relationships with other
 stakeholders.
