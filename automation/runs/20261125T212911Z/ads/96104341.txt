The role
McIlwain is seeking a Site Engineer / Project Engineer to join our growing NSW team on a full-time, permanent basis. With more than 60 years of road infrastructure delivery in Queensland, we've recently set up our NSW business. We're pursuing Transport for NSW (TfNSW) projects across Greater Sydney, the Hunter and the Illawarra.
You'll start in our North Sydney office, supporting tenders, planning and procurement and reporting to the Regional Manager - NSW. Once we win a project, you'll move to site and report to the Project Manager, managing day-to-day engineering, quality and site coordination. Projects include Construct Only and Design & Construct contracts, including joint venture delivery.
Our NSW office is a short walk from both North Sydney train station and Victoria Cross metro station. This is a great chance to join a new business unit early, see projects through from tender to handover, and grow your career towards Project Manager.
Responsibilities 
In the office (tender and pre-construction):
- Assist with quantity take-offs and review tender drawings and specifications
- Provide input on construction methodology, staging and temporary works
- Help identify construction risks, constraints and opportunities for the bid team
- Support tender and early delivery programmes and resource planning
- Assist with procurement, including getting and comparing subcontractor and supplier quotes
On site (project delivery):
- Plan and coordinate daily and weekly site activities with supervisors, subcontractors and suppliers
- Manage set-out, survey coordination and checks against design
- Run the quality system, including ITPs, hold and witness points, test results and lot records to TfNSW specifications
- Prepare and review SWMS, traffic guidance schemes, road occupancy licences and environmental controls
- Track quantities and progress, and support the Project Manager with progress claims, variations and look-ahead programmes
- Manage RFIs, design queries, and interfaces with utilities, councils and the community
- Prepare as-constructed records and handover documentation, and close out defects
What we're looking for 
- Tertiary qualification in civil engineering
- At least 4 years' experience as a Site or Project Engineer with a civil contractor
- Experience on TfNSW (or former RMS) road or civil projects
- Road infrastructure experience such as road upgrades, intersections, pavements, earthworks, drainage or bridges, ideally brownfield work under live traffic
- Working knowledge of TfNSW QA specifications, ITPs, hold points, lot management and conformance records
- Experience coordinating subcontractors, plant and crews to a look-ahead programme
- Experience preparing or reviewing SWMS and working under TfNSW traffic and environmental requirements
- Experience tracking quantities and supporting progress claims and variations
- Tender support experience for a road authority, highly regarded
- Comfortable working in a new business unit with a small team, and keen to develop towards a Project Manager role
- Willing to travel to regional NSW sites
- Current NSW driver's licence and General Construction Induction (White Card)
