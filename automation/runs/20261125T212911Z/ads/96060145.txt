About this role

This is a hands-on role offering variety, autonomy and the opportunity to make a real impact by collaborating with customers, engineers, production teams and contractors to deliver innovative projects safely, efficiently and to the highest standard.

- Manage project scope, budgets, schedules, risks and stakeholder communication.
- Coordinate clients, consultants, subcontractors and internal teams to achieve successful project outcomes.
- Support tendering, estimating and value-engineering initiatives.
- Ensure projects meet quality, safety and compliance requirements.
- Provide technical engineering support across project delivery and manufacturing operations.
- Drive continuous improvement initiatives that enhance project performance, efficiency and customer satisfaction.

- Degree in Civil or Structural Engineering

About Smartstream Technology
Smartstream Technology are an Australian family-owned company, specialising in civil engineering infrastructure solutions using roto moulding and plastic injection technology as well as precast concrete to produce a highly developed range of wastewater and stormwater products. We are innovators in civil construction, dedicated to investigating new processes and materials with a range of unique products.
