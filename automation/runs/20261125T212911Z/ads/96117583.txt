Are you an experience Draftsperson with strong SolidWorks skills looking for a role that offers flexibility without compromising on interesting, hands-on engineering work?
Willweld is seeking a motivated and detail orientated Draftsperson to join our team, supporting engineering and design projects within the mining industry.
This is an excellent opportunity for someone who enjoys working independently, producing high-quality engineering drawings and being a part of a drafting team that delivers practical solutions to the resources sector.
The Opportunity
Enjoy a genuinely flexible working arrangement:
- 3 weeks working from home.
- 1 week on site – working alongside the team and engaging with projects first-hand.
This arrangement provides the flexibility of remote working combined with regular face-to-face collaboration and exposure to site operations.
What you’ll be doing.
As a member of the team, you will assist with:
- Designing and 3D/2D modelling of new assemblies and parts as per requirements.
- Creating detailed fabrication drawings for production.
- Updating and revising of existing assemblies and drawings when required.
- Liaising with workshop personnel and managers in implementing new design.
- Attending weekly team meetings.
- Maintaining drawing register with up-to-date drawings.
- Obtain quotes for all components, finished products and services.
- Raise, place and monitor purchase orders.
- Working alongside Project Lead Draftsperson to assist in set up of work packs and job management.
- Assist in all other areas when required to support our busy team.
- Some site visits maybe required.
 About you.
- Have 2+ years’ experience in product designing, 3D/2D modelling new assemblies and parts.
- SolidWorks proficiency; employing parametric design intent, sheet metal & weldment features competency, ability to manage multibody and large assembly files.
- Experience with Microsoft Office software programs essential.
- Have previous purchasing experience – (preferred).
- Highly motivated self-starter and strong problem-solving skills. 
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills.
