 We're seeking a Project Engineer to deliver road, bridge and traffic infrastructure construction and maintenance projects. Based in Holbrook, you'll coordinate projects from planning through to completion, ensuring works meet agreed time, budget, safety and quality requirements.
 Key responsibilities
 - Prepare project briefs, cost estimates, work programs and delivery schedules.
 - Coordinate investigations and design activities, overseeing consultants and service providers.
 - Manage construction and maintenance works delivered by internal crews and external contractors.
 - Prepare tender documentation, technical specifications and contracts.
 - Monitor project expenditure, progress and contractor performance.
 - Implement safety, quality, environmental and traffic management requirements.
 - Work with stakeholders to resolve technical issues and manage delivery expectations.
 About you
 - Previous NSW Government experience.
 - A four-year degree in Civil, Electrical or a related engineering discipline, or an equivalent overseas qualification.
 - Experience delivering road, bridge or traffic infrastructure construction and maintenance projects.
 - Sound knowledge of construction materials, equipment and methods.
 - Experience preparing estimates, work programs and project management plans.
 - Experience coordinating construction crews, managing contractors and administering contracts.
 - Strong communication, stakeholder management and problem-solving skills.
 - A current Australian driver's licence and willingness to travel occasionally.
