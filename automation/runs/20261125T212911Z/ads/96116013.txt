Burton Contractors is seeking a skilled and driven Project Engineer to join our team. If you have proven experience in bulk earthworks, roadworks and civil infrastructure, and are passionate about delivering high-quality outcomes, we welcome the opportunity to hear from you.
Reporting directly to the Project Manager, the Project Engineer will play a key role in coordinating project activities, providing technical support and ensuring works are delivered safely, efficiently and in line with project programs and specifications.
As a Project Engineer, you will be expected to:
- Provide technical support and guidance across project activities, ensuring compliance with design specifications and construction requirements.
- Manage project administration and work closely with the Project Manager and Site Supervisors to coordinate site activities.
- Coordinate subcontractors, monitor construction progress and assist in achieving project milestones.
- Assist with cost management, including unit cost analysis, progress claims and contractual notices.
- Maintain compliance with WHS, environmental and quality requirements.
Essential Competencies:
- Civil Engineering Degree or equivalent qualification.
- Proven experience as a Project Engineer within the civil construction industry.
- Bulk earthworks, roadworks and civil infrastructure project experience.
- Comprehensive knowledge of Council and TfNSW specifications.
- Ability to read and interpret engineering drawings and plans.
- Experience in subcontractor coordination and project administration.
- Strong planning, organisational and problem-solving abilities.
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
- Understanding of WHS, Environmental and Quality Management Systems.
This is an exciting opportunity for a motivated and organised individual to apply their technical expertise to significant civil construction projects, work alongside an experienced team and contribute to successful project outcomes.
Burton Contractors offers a professional and collaborative working environment where initiative is valued and individual contributions make a meaningful difference.
An attractive salary package will be negotiated with the successful applicant, commensurate with experience.
