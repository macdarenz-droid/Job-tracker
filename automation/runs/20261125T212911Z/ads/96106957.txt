Why Join Our Team?
Based in Perth, you’ll join Ausenco’s global Engineering Applications Solutions team and primarily support engineering and design teams across Asia-Pacific (APAC). Your main focus will be Autodesk Forma, including Data Management, Design Collaboration and model coordination workflows familiar to Autodesk Construction Cloud (ACC) users. You’ll also support AutoCAD, Plant 3D and Advance Steel.
What You Will Be Doing
- Administer Autodesk applications, troubleshoot software and installation issues, and work with Autodesk or other vendors to resolve complex cases.
- Support project teams with controlled information sharing, model review, multidisciplinary coordination and issue tracking.
- Configure project environments, templates, specifications and catalogues in line with approved engineering standards and regional requirements.
- Onboard and train users, maintain practical guidance, and work with Engineering, Design and IT to improve workflows and identify opportunities for automation.
You Will Bring
- Hands-on experience supporting or developing solutions in Autodesk Forma or ACC, including data management, design collaboration and model coordination.
- Experience supporting AutoCAD and configuring, administering or supporting Plant 3D, Advance Steel or both.
- A strong understanding of engineering and CAD/BIM workflows and the ability to explain technical issues clearly and manage competing requests.
- A post-secondary diploma or certificate in IT, computer science, engineering technology or a related discipline. A relevant bachelor’s degree is highly regarded.
