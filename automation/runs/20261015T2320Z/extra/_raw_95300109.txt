About the role
Auspro is seeking a suitably qualified and experienced Engineering Draftsperson to join our engineering team. The role involves preparing detailed engineering drawings and documentation to support infrastructure and construction projects, including roads, drainage, and subdivisions.
Key responsibilities
- Prepare detailed engineering drawings, plans, and layouts using CAD software
- Assist in the design and documentation of projects
- Interpret engineering sketches, specifications, and calculations
- Update and revise drawings based on design changes and project requirements
- Ensure drawings comply with Australian standards, codes, and regulations
- Liaise with engineers, surveyors, and project stakeholders as required
- Maintain accurate drawing registers and documentation records
About you
- Demonstrated experience as an Engineering Draftsperson or similar role
- Proficiency in AutoCAD (or equivalent civil drafting software)
- Ability to read and interpret engineering plans and technical specifications
- Knowledge of Australian engineering standards and practices
- Strong attention to detail and time management skills
- Ability to work independently and as part of a team
- Diploma or Advanced Diploma in Engineering Drafting, Civil Engineering, or a related field (or equivalent qualification)
