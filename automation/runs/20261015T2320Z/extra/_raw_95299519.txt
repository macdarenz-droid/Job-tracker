WE'RE HIRING
Designer / Drafter (Façade)
Rickard Engineering – Sydney
Significant project wins mean we are expanding our Sydney team. We are seeking an experienced and driven Designer / Drafter to join our award-winning consultancy. This role offers the opportunity to work on technically complex façade and structural projects both locally and internationally, as part of a highly collaborative and innovative multi-disciplinary team. The position involves leading the technical drafting and modelling delivery of façade and structural packages, working closely with Engineers and supporting Junior Drafters to ensure high-quality outcomes across all project stages — from concept development through to construction and site inspections.
The Role offers the successful applicant a wide variety of opportunities and duties, including:
• Leading the production of high-quality 2D drawings and 3D models for façade and structural systems, including steel, concrete, timber, glazing, and composite assemblies.
• Working on interstate and international projects across a diverse range of building types.
• Providing technical guidance and mentoring to Junior Drafters, reviewing outputs, and assisting with resource planning and workflow coordination.
• Liaising directly with Engineers and Clients to support smooth project delivery and ensure deadlines are met.
• Developing and maintaining company CAD/BIM standards, templates, libraries, and document control procedures.
• Coordinating drawing packages across multiple disciplines and managing consultant interfaces.
• Participating in site inspections, project meetings, and contributing to technical documentation and reports.
Qualifications, Skills, and Experience:
• Tertiary qualification or trade/technical background in Drafting, Architecture, Engineering, or a related discipline.
• Minimum 3+ years of relevant experience in façades with senior-level responsibility.
• Strong experience across a wide range of façade systems, including but not limited to glazing systems (window wall, curtain wall, stick system, skylights, etc.), metal cladding, masonry, CFC, terracotta/ceramic, composite facades & associated interfaces.
• Experience in DBPA project drafting will be considered advantageous.
• Experience in structural drafting will be considered advantageous.
• Demonstrated experience preparing detailed structural drawings for steel, reinforced and post-tensioned concrete, and timber structures.
• Advanced proficiency in AutoCAD and 3D modelling software (e.g. Revit, Rhino, or similar).
• Strong understanding of construction methodologies, shop drawing standards, and buildability considerations.
• Ability to interpret engineering concepts, design intent, and technical specifications accurately.
• Strong organisational skills with the ability to manage multiple projects and priorities concurrently.
• Excellent communication skills and the ability to collaborate effectively within a team environment.
• Working rights in Australia are required.
Only shortlisted applicants will be contacted.
