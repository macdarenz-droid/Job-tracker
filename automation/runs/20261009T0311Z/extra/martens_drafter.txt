 Civil Drafter – Junior to Mid Level
 at 
 Martens & Associates Pty Ltd 
 Hornsby, New South Wales 2077 , Australia • Full-time 
 AI Job Summary 
 Demonstrable high-level 2D drafting skills with BricsCAD or AutoCAD. 
 Civil drafting experience in Australian land development, including large drawing sets and drawing-set tools. 
 Full-time Australian work rights (no restricted visa holders). 
 Sign up or log in to analyse my fit
 Role Type
 On-site • Permanent • Full-time • Entry to Mid Level 
 Description
 Why Martens? 
 Are you looking to become a member of a growing, vibrant and well established multidisciplinary professional engineering consulting firm? Are you searching for opportunities to develop your civil drafting skills and applying these to achieve high quality outcomes?
 Martens & Associates, established since 1989, is a well established multidisciplinary professional consulting firm that provides a full range of civil, geotechnical and environmental engineering and town planning services. Our business is built on the quality of our staff and our flexible and friendly work environment. We believe that the skills, capability and enthusiasm of our people give us our competitive advantage.
 About The Role 
 We are seeking a talented junior to intermediate level Civil Drafter (AutoCAD 2d) to become a valued member of our busy multidisciplinary engineering office based in Hornsby, NSW.
 The role presents an excellent opportunity for an experienced civil drafter wishing to build on their existing career. It is also suitable for willing and enthusiastic new or recently qualified drafters, engineers, and engineering students wanting to build their experience.
 It involves working from our Hornsby office on projects with a wide range of civil engineering works including:
 Property and development drainage assessment.
 Development of site water quality, quantity and drainage solutions.
 Civil engineering design of earthworks, roads, regrading, etc.
 Flood assessments, remediation and management plans.
 Competitive salary on offer, negotiable on the basis of experience.
 About You 
 We are seeking candidates that take pride in their work, have a positive attitude, are punctual, reliable, hardworking and able to work in a dynamic and fast paced environment.
 It is essential for the applicants to have:
 Good verbal and written communications skills in English.
 The ability to work in a culturally diverse and multi disciplinary team environment.
 Positive and proactive attitude and be motivated, team player, organised and eager to learn.
 Full time Australian work rights (no restricted visa holders).
 Demonstrable high level 2d drafting skills with BricsCAD or AutoCAD.
 Shortlisted applicants will be asked to provide copies of past relevant works and/or showcase their drafting skills at the interview.
 Any of the following experiences and qualities will be considered favourable :
 Previous drafting experience with large drawing sets (using xref, layer state, annotation scale, paper space, publish, sheet set, etc.), preferably as a civil drafter in land development projects in Australia.
 Familiar with the general process of land development (DA, CC, etc.) and civil engineering design components (site analysis, earthworks, roads, drainage, etc.).
 Experience with GIS software packages (QGIS, ArcGIS, etc.).
 Experience with Lisp coding for AutoCAD and/or Python scripting for QGIS.
 How to Apply 
 If you are interested in this opportunity and meet the above requirements, please apply:
 Preferably, through this post.
 Alternatively, by sending an email to careers@martens.com.au with [CivilDrafter260922] and your full name in the email subject.
 Please provide your cover letter, resume, all relevant academic transcripts to date, and contactable work referees, as part of your application.
 We encourage direct applications (preferably not via recruiters ).
