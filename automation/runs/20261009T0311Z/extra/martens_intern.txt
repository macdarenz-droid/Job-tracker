 Description
 INTERN CIVIL DESIGN ENGINEER 
 $60k+ based on experience and skills
 Well established and growing company.
 Opportunity for diverse and long term career progression.
 Flexible, friendly and supportive work environment with training program.
 Exciting opportunity for an intern/graduate to intermediate Civil Design Engineer to join Martens & Associates’ team of motivated professionals and work on a wide range of civil engineering projects. The position is based in Hornsby in Sydney’s north.
 WHY MARTENS? 
 Are you looking to join a growing, vibrant and well established multidisciplinary professional engineering consulting firm? Are you searching for opportunities to develop your civil design knowledge and skills and applying these to achieve high quality outcomes in a wide range of civil engineering projects?
 Martens & Associates, established since 1989, is a well established multidisciplinary professional consulting firm that provides a full range of civil, geotechnical and environmental engineering and town planning services. Our business is built on the quality of our staff and our flexible, friendly and supportive work environment. We believe that the skills, capability and enthusiasm of our people give us our competitive advantage.
 ABOUT THE POSITION 
 We are seeking a talented intern / graduate to intermediate level Civil Design Engineer to join our busy multidisciplinary engineering office based in Hornsby, NSW.
 The position presents an excellent opportunity to be exposed to a busy professional engineering consultancy with a wide range of projects. It is also an excellent opportunity to start a career with successful interns offered an opportunity for continued employment.
 The intern position will provide the successful candidate exposure to engineering projects, involving a wide range of engineering works including:
 Flood assessments, remediation and management plans.
 Property and development drainage assessment.
 Development of site water quality, quantity and drainage solutions.
 Civil engineering design of earthworks, roads, regrading, etc.
 Internships are offered on a 6 month full time basis. On successful completion of internships, opportunities may exist for continued full time employment while finalising studies.
 ABOUT YOU 
 We are seeking candidates that take pride in their work, have a positive attitude, are punctual, reliable, hardworking and able to work in a dynamic and fast paced environment.
 It is ESSENTIAL for the applicants to:
 Be graduated from or enrolled in the later part of an engineering degree.
 Have good written and verbal communications skills in English.
 Be motivated, team player, well organised, and eager to learn.
 Have the ability to work in a civil design team within a culturally diverse office.
 It is FAVOURABLE for the applicants to have any of the following experiences and qualities:
 Civil design and modelling software packages (12d Model, Civil 3d, etc).
 Hydraulic, hydrological and water quality models (TUFLOW, DRAINS, HEC-RAS, RAFTS, MUSIC, 12d Model’s DNE, etc).
 GIS software packages (QGIS, ArcGIS, etc).
 CAD software packages (AutoCAD, BricsCAD, etc).
 Computer programming and scripting (Python, PyQGIS, VBA, C++, AutoLISP, etc).
 Previous experience in multi disciplinary consultancy, especially in land development.
 RESIDENCY REQUIREMENTS: All candidates considered including international students.
 HOW TO APPLY 
 If you are interested in this opportunity and meet the above requirements, please apply:
 Preferably, through this post.
 Alternatively, by sending an email to careers@martens.com.au with [ InternCivil260805 ] and your full name in the email subject.
 Please provide your cover letter, attn Soroush Ziaee, and your resume, with details of work experiences and all relevant up to date university transcripts, as part of your application.
 Company Overview
 Martens & Associates, established since 1989, is a well established multidisciplinary professional consulting firm that provides a full range of civil, geotechnical and environmental engineering and town planning services. Our business is built on the quality of our staff and our flexible and friendly work environment. We believe that the skills, capability and enthusiasm of our people give us our competitive advantage.
