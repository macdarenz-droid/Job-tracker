 Expression of Interest
 at 
 Martens & Associates Pty Ltd 
 Hornsby, New South Wales 2077 , Australia • Full-time 
 AI Job Summary 
 Experience in civil engineering (listed as a desired discipline). 
 Candidates from intern and graduate to principal level across listed disciplines encouraged to apply. 
 Provide cover letter, resume, academic transcripts and contactable work referees with EOI. 
 Sign up or log in to analyse my fit
 Role Type
 On-site • Permanent • Full-time • All Levels 
 Description
 Why Martens? 
 Are you looking to join a growing, vibrant and well established multidisciplinary professional engineering consulting firm? Are you searching for opportunities to develop your technical knowledge and skills and applying these to achieve high quality outcomes in a wide range of engineering projects?
 Martens & Associates, since 1989, is a well established multidisciplinary professional consulting firm that provides a full range of civil, geotechnical and environmental engineering and town planning services. Our business is built on the quality of our staff and our flexible, friendly and supportive work environment. We believe that the skills, capability and enthusiasm of our people give us our competitive advantage.
 About You 
 We are inviting Expressions of Interest from talented interns and graduate to principal level professionals across a range of disciplines, including:
 Civil Engineers.
 Geotechnical Engineers.
 Environmental Engineers/Consultants.
 Town Planners.
 Project Managers.
 You take pride in your work and have a positive attitude. You are punctual, reliable, and hardworking. You are able to work in a dynamic and fast paced environment. And, you are keen to become a valued member of our busy multidisciplinary engineering office based in Hornsby. If this sounds like you, we would love to hear from you! 
 How to Express Your Interest 
 Please submit your EIO:
 Preferably, through this post.
 Alternatively, by emailing careers@martens.com.au with “EOI – [Your Full Name] – [Your Discipline]” in the subject line.
 Please provide your cover letter, resume (outlining your discipline, expertise, and experience), all relevant academic transcripts to date, and contactable work referees, as part of your EOI.
 We encourage direct EOIs (preferably not via recruiters ).
 Company Overview
 Martens & Associates, established since 1989, is a well established multidisciplinary professional consulting firm that provides a full range of civil, geotechnical and environmental engineering and town planning services. Our business is built on the quality of our staff and our flexible and friendly work environment. We believe that the skills, capability and enthusiasm of our people give us our competitive advantage.
