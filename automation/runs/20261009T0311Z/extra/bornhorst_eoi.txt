 Join the Bornhorst + Ward Team 
 Are you passionate about engineering, drafting, or supporting innovative projects? Bornhorst + Ward, a trusted name in civil and structural engineering for 60 years, welcomes expressions of interest from individuals keen to join our dynamic team.
 Whether you’re an experienced professional or just starting your career, we offer opportunities across a range of roles in civil and structural engineering, drafting, and support functions. Our team works on exciting and impactful engineering projects in a supportive environment dedicated to professional growth and development.
 Key Responsibilities 
Depending on your area of expertise, your responsibilities may include:
 Technical Contributions: Work on engineering designs, technical drawings, and project planning.
 Software Proficiency: Use industry-leading tools like AutoCAD, Revit, and other BIM software.
 Collaboration: Engage with a multidisciplinary team to deliver efficient, innovative solutions.
 Site Visits: Contribute to real-world projects by gathering data and supporting on-site activities.
 Continuous Improvement: Update designs or processes based on feedback and project needs.
 About You 
We’re seeking enthusiastic and motivated individuals who have:
 An interest in the engineering and construction industry.
 Strong communication skills and a proactive, problem-solving mindset.
 A willingness to learn, adapt, and contribute to innovative projects.
 Technical skills or experience with CAD software (AutoCAD, Revit, or similar) are a plus.
 The ability to work collaboratively within a team-oriented culture.
 Whether you’re a recent graduate, an experienced professional, or someone looking to transition into a new career path, we’d love to hear from you.
 Why Join Bornhorst + Ward? 
 At Bornhorst + Ward, we believe a fulfilling career is about being part of a connected and motivated team that knows how to work hard and have fun.
 Professional Growth & Development : Access mentorship, ongoing training, and RPEQ fee coverage, along with industry-specific programs to support your career progression.
 Comprehensive Insurance & Security : Enjoy peace of mind with Group Income Protection and Life Insurance policies.
 Work-Life Balance & Flexibility : Flexible hours, remote work options, and self-managed schedules let you balance work and personal life.
 Modern Work Environment : Work in our industrial-inspired CBD office with river views, close to transport.
 Perks & Facilities : Enjoy free Nespresso coffee, breakfast, soft drinks, seasonal fruit, and end-of-trip facilities for active lifestyles.
 Team Culture: Regular social events including trivia nights, pub crawls, barefoot lawn bowls, and even camping trips.
 Wellness & Community: Join our run club, show off your skills in baking competitions, or get involved in team-building and fundraising events that give back to the community.
 Unique Incentives : Alongside a competitive salary, win rewards like 1 kg of silver bullion at our annual Christmas event and benefit from extended leave for long-standing employees.
 Ready to Apply?
