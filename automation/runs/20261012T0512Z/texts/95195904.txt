Alchemy Recruitment is partnering with a State Government client to recruit four engineering roles in Nerang, Gold Coast. You’ll join one of two teams managing more than 1,000 km of national highway and state-controlled roads across the Gold Coast, Logan and Scenic Rim. The Preservation and Maintenance team delivers maintenance, rehabilitation and road safety projects, while the Asset Management team assesses road condition and plans future works. With roles available from Student through to Principal level, this is a great opportunity to join a growing region.
 About the role
 - Pay rate of between $88.30 and $94.59 per hour + super (Principal Engineer, PO5), between $78.63 and $84.56 per hour + super (Senior Civil Engineer, PO4), between $67.95 and $74.00 per hour + super (Civil Engineer, PO3), or between $32.11 and $47.35 per hour + super (Student Engineer, PO1)
 - 12-month contracts starting early November 2026, with possible extension
 - Flexible full-time hours for the PO3, PO4 and PO5 roles; 2 to 3 days per week during semester and full-time over university breaks for the Student Engineer
 - Based at the regional office in Nerang, with regular site work across the region
 - Work on a varied program of road maintenance, pavement rehabilitation, resurfacing, bridge maintenance, asset condition assessment and road safety projects
 - Work closely with Road Operations, contractors, consultants, local councils and other government agencies
 - Contribute to safety leadership and quality outcomes on every project
 Principal Engineer (PO5) 
 - Act as Representative of the Principal for the delivery of contracts across each project phase
 - Develop contract documentation, manage tender calls and assessment (including design and construct), and lead contract award for projects in the region’s roads program
 - Project manage design and construction through the full lifecycle, including on urban and multi-lane high-speed roads
 - Report on progress and actual and forecast expenditure
 - Ensure quality and environmental requirements are identified and met
 - Supervise, allocate and mentor engineering and technical staff
 - Manage stakeholder expectations and identify political and high-risk matters early
 Senior Civil Engineer (PO4) 
 - Act as project manager and contract administrator on road projects from pre-construction through to construction
 - Manage preliminary investigations including survey, geotechnical and land resumption requirements
 - Take projects from concept design through detailed design and tender
 - Assess construction tenders, manage procurement and administer complex contracts
 - Deliver and report on Targeted Road Safety Program works and maintenance, preservation and rehabilitation programs
 - Supervise and mentor engineers, graduates and technical staff
 - Provide technical solutions during natural disaster and emergency events affecting the network
 Civil Engineer (PO3) 
 - Project manage road and bridge maintenance and rehabilitation works under the guidance of senior engineers
 - Assist in selecting the right contract model, then administer contracts through to completion
 - Set road and bridge maintenance procedures and work instructions
 - Calculate material quantities, review test results and manage non-conformances
 - Conduct risk assessments and determine construction controls
 - Inspect works and release hold points during construction and maintenance
 - Provide input into planning and design, including negotiating changes to design briefs
 Student Engineer (PO1) 
 - Join the Asset Management team under the direct supervision of a Senior Engineer
 - Investigate road asset condition and use data analytics to identify issues and solutions across the network
 - Contribute to maintenance programs and plans covering pavements, resurfacing, structures, drainage, batter slopes, heavy vehicle permits, signage and delineation
 - Assist with condition analysis and forward program preparation
 - Help prepare technical reports, stakeholder advice and replies to correspondence
 - Get out on site across the region and build practical experience alongside your studies
 Preferred Requirements 
 - Road infrastructure delivery experience at the relevant level, from state government, local council, contracting or consulting (PO3, PO4 and PO5)
 - Contract administration, superintendent or principal’s representative experience on civil works (PO3, PO4 and PO5)
 - Tender preparation and assessment experience (PO4 and PO5)
 - Experience across both pre-construction and construction phases (PO4 and PO5)
 - Leadership, supervision and mentoring of engineers (PO4 and PO5)
 - Experience on urban and multi-lane high-speed roads (PO5)
 - Materials testing review, non-conformance management and hold point release (PO3)
 - Genuine interest in road and transport infrastructure, and comfortable working with data and spreadsheets (Student Engineer)
 - Ability to commit to regular days during semester (Student Engineer)
 - Familiarity with MRTS specifications or road authority forms of contract
 - Disaster recovery or emergency works experience highly regarded
 - Sound judgement, rigorous analysis and strong stakeholder relationships
 - Clear written and verbal communication
 - Willing to undergo a criminal history check (essential, all roles)
 Preferred Qualifications 
 - Registered Professional Engineer of Queensland (RPEQ), Civil (essential for PO4 and PO5, highly regarded for PO3)
 - Four-year, Washington Accord accredited Bachelor of Engineering (Civil), or equivalent assessed by Engineers Australia (essential for PO3, PO4 and PO5)
 - Currently enrolled in second or third year of a four-year, Washington Accord accredited Bachelor of Engineering (Civil) (essential for Student Engineer)
 - Current Class C driver’s licence (essential, all roles)
 - Construction Industry Induction (White Card) (essential for PO3, PO4 and PO5)
