Summary:
As the Site Engineer in the Rockhampton based office, you will be working alongside the Project Manager on delivering civil asset renewal and maintenance projects in the rail sector, as well as other civil infrastructure projects. The role is permanent/full time and will be a mix of office and field based workloads managing contractors, ensuring quality compliance and project management. You will be required to travel throughout Northern/Central Queensland and surrounding regions.
Responsibilities:
- Ensuring safe and efficient delivery including managing works to meet client expectations and industry / regulated safety and technical standards
- Preparation of project documentation including safety management plans, schedules, and other project specific documents
- Managing project deliverables by project coordination, scheduling, and administration
- Quality assurance and control
- Ensure site conforms to HSE policies and procedures
- Internal reporting and job management
- Stakeholder communication within boundaries of the projects
- Management of project contractors and subcontractors
- Procurement of required work permits, approvals and project materials
Qualifications:
- Bachelor's degree in civil engineering or similar
- 2+ year's experience in an equivalent or similar role
- Class C Driver’s Licence
- Construction Industry Induction (White Card)
- Strong interpersonal and written skills to communicate with all stakeholders
- High level of organisational & time management skills
- Rail, civil construction and/or project management experience
- RIW competency
