About the role
The Drafter role is an intermediate level position, reporting to the National Drafting Manager and management staff in the Melbourne office. As part of the Drafting and Design Team based at the Sydney facility in Warwick Farm, you will be designing and drawing innovative structures for events held nationally and globally. Training and on-going guidance are provided.
Key responsibilities
- Preparing drawings and technical plans that comply with customer specifications, BCA standards, and business drafting guidelines
- Liaising with the production, operations, and fabrication teams to ensure structures are practical to build and make the most of equipment in stock
- Occasionally interfacing with customers to clarify their requirements
- Attending site periodically to gain a feel for the event and structures being designed
- Visiting the warehouse to familiarise yourself with the equipment being drawn
- Document preparation and document management
- Assisting with general business and office activities, as directed
About you
- Experience in AutoCAD for 2D and 3D modelling
- A flair for calculations and problem solving, with a passion for designing innovative practical structures
- Sound time management skills and the ability to work accurately while adhering to strict deadlines
- Exceptional communication skills - listening, written and verbal
- Ability to learn quickly and to work autonomously and as part of a team
- A 'can do' attitude, good attention to detail and pride in your work
- Proficiency in Microsoft Office (Outlook, Excel, Word) and Google Chrome, with knowledge of Dropbox and Adobe Acrobat
- Australian Citizenship or Permanent Residency, with a valid working visa and Tax File Number
About us
Clifton Productions is a leading supplier in the Event industry supplying concert staging, temporary seating and temporary structures for events in the Australian and International market. Head office is in Melbourne, with fully staffed offices and warehouses in Melbourne, Sydney and Brisbane. Our national drafting division is a small dedicated team based at our Sydney facility in Warwick Farm.
