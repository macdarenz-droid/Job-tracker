RoadTek is a commercial business within the Department of Transport and Main Roads (TMR), delivering transport infrastructure solutions across Queensland. We value safety, respect, diversity and inclusion and provide opportunities for our people to grow and develop.
About the role:
As Project Coordinator based in Winton, you will plan and coordinate multi-functional work crews across civil infrastructure projects, supporting the efficient delivery of the works program.
Working with Project Managers and Supervisors, you will coordinate resources, subcontractors, suppliers and internal teams, assist with short-term works programming, monitor project performance and risks, and support compliance with safety, environmental and quality requirements.
You will contribute to continuous improvement, support effective team performance, mentor project staff, maintain strong communication across projects and assist with tendering and estimating activities.
Why RoadTek?
Build your career with RoadTek while enjoying everything regional Queensland has to offer. You'll play a part in delivering important transport infrastructure, supported by TMR's focus on development, wellbeing and work-life balance.
Winton offers more than a great career opportunity. With shorter commutes, affordable housing, a relaxed lifestyle and a strong sense of community, you can enjoy more time for what matters while building genuine connections and making an mpact through your work.

About you:
You will bring strong planning and organisational skills, sound judgement and the ability to apply project management principles to road infrastructure activities. You will work collaboratively to achieve project outcomes while managing risks and operating within relevant legislation, policies and standards.
• Applicants must hold a current Queensland C class driver licence or equivalent and be able to undertake travel as required.
• A Certificate IV in Project Management and an accredited Frontline Management qualification are desirable.
