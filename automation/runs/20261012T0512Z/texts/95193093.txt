About the Company
Hall Contracting is recognised throughout Australia, the Pacific, and Asia as specialists in the dredging and marine industries. They have grown to become one of the largest contractors of their kind in Australia and consistently deliver high-quality results for their stakeholders.
With award-winning capabilities in dredging, civil contracting, and marine engineering, they pride themselves on their expertise in project management and their strong commitment to clients, employees, and the wider community.
 The Opportunity
 Hall Contracting are continuing to grow their team and are looking to take on 2x Undergraduate Engineers. You will gain invaluable engineering and construction knowledge whilst working alongside an experienced Pre-contract dredging and maritime team at our Sunshine Coast office.
 You will have an opportunity to support Hall’s preparation for a wide range of dredging and maritime projects, from small local coastal protection and beach renourishments jobs to Australia’s largest Port infrastructure developments.
 Key Responsibilities
 The responsibilities of this role will vary with the prospective projects, however we expect our undergraduates to bring their engineering and problem-solving skills to gain invaluable knowledge and then complete the following tasks:
 - Create and analyse 3D surfaces and geotechnical ground models (Civil 3D).
 - Prepare geotechnical site assessments (digitise bore logs, CPT’s and geophysical data).
 - Complete pump capacity assessments (pumping head).
 - Undertake production calculations for large dredging equipment.
 - Complete conceptual designs of marine structures.
 - Provide CAD drafting and 3D modelling services.
 - Generate and adjust delivery programs (MS Project).
 - Provide academic research and annotated bibliographies.
 - Reviewing and summarising technical documentations.
 - Complete research on construction markets.
