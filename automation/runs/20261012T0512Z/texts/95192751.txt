Graduate Structural Engineer | Remedial Consultancy | Pyrmont
Job Summary
Join a growing Sydney engineering consultancy specialising in remedial, structural and façade engineering. An excellent opportunity for a Civil Engineering graduate with an interest in structures to gain practical experience, work alongside experienced engineers and develop their career.
About the Business
Our client is a well-established and growing engineering consultancy based in Pyrmont, specialising in remedial engineering, façade remediation and structural rehabilitation projects.
The business has developed a strong reputation through its technical expertise and long-standing client relationships, working across residential, commercial and government projects.
Their portfolio includes remedial, façade and structural rehabilitation projects ranging from approximately $2 million to $15 million+, providing excellent exposure to different buildings, structural systems and engineering challenges.
With continued growth, they're looking to appoint a Graduate Engineer who can develop alongside their experienced engineering team.
The Opportunity
This is an excellent opportunity for a recent Civil Engineering graduate who has a particular interest in structural engineering and wants to develop their technical skills within a consultancy environment.
You'll work closely with experienced engineers, gaining exposure to building inspections, structural investigations, defect assessments, remedial design and construction.
Rather than being limited to office-based design work, you'll have the opportunity to develop an understanding of how buildings perform, why defects occur and how practical engineering solutions are developed and implemented.
The business is looking for someone with a great attitude, strong willingness to learn and genuine ambition to build a long-term career in engineering.
Key Responsibilities
- Assist senior engineers with structural and remedial engineering projects
- Support building inspections, site investigations and defect assessments
- Assist with structural assessments and engineering calculations
- Support the preparation of technical reports and remedial scopes of work
- Assist with structural and remedial design documentation
- Coordinate with engineers, consultants and contractors
- Attend site inspections and develop practical construction knowledge
- Support project delivery and maintain accurate technical documentation
About You
You'll ideally have:
- Completed Bachelor's Degree in Civil Engineering
- Ideally 1-2 years post graduate experience
- A strong interest in structural engineering, ideally with relevant structural subjects or project experience
- Good understanding of structural engineering fundamentals
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
- Excellent attention to detail
- Good written and verbal communication skills
- Positive attitude and willingness to learn
- Professional, personable and proactive approach
- Genuine interest in remedial engineering and building structures
Previous internship or work experience within structural engineering, construction or building consultancy would be advantageous but isn't essential.
Recent graduates are encouraged to apply. Attitude, communication and willingness to develop are particularly important.
