Nestled between rolling hills and some of the state’s most stunning beaches, this region is home to a variety of people who enjoy both rural and coastal living.

 The Opportunity
We have an exciting opportunity for a motivated individual to join Councils Emerging Talent Program and undertake an undergraduate qualification in Engineering (honours) or a related discipline while gaining practical experience and mentorship across Council's Infrastructure Directorate.
As an Engineer Cadet, you will combine academic studies with hands-on workplace experience across a range of areas including Works and Projects, Water and Sewer Services, Asset Management, Design and Technical Services. You will gain exposure to infrastructure planning, project delivery, asset management, engineering design and technical services while developing practical skills within a local government environment.
The role may also involve travel and participation in residential study blocks associated with your academic program.
As a participant in Council's Emerging Talent Program, you will have the opportunity to develop your skills through structured learning, on-the-job experience, mentoring and exposure to a broad range of professional disciplines, building a strong foundation for a successful career in local government and beyond.
Council will support your studies by providing one paid study day per week during each university semester or trimester, together with funding for approved course fees and study-related expenses.
Key responsibilities include:
 - Assist in the delivery of engineering services across a range of functions, including project management, asset management, engineering design, inspections and technical services.
 - Assist with the planning, delivery, monitoring and evaluation of engineering projects and programs that support Council's infrastructure network.
 - Develop practical skills in data analysis, technical investigations, reporting, surveying, site inspections and project documentation.
 - Contribute to the delivery of engineering services that comply with statutory requirements, relevant standards and Council policies and procedures.
 - Apply your university learning in a practical environment while developing the technical, analytical and professional skills required for a successful career in engineering.
 This role offers valuable exposure to a broad range of infrastructure services while developing practical skills and experience to support a future career in engineering.

About You
We are seeking an individual who holds a Higher School Certificate (or equivalent) and is eligible to enrol in an Engineering (Honours) degree.
You will have the following skills and experience;
 - Demonstrated interest in engineering, infrastructure, science or related technical disciplines.
 - Good written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to liaise effectively with internal and external stakeholders.
 - Demonstrated experience using the Microsoft Office suite.
 - Analytical thinking and the ability to solve problems logically and systematically.
 - Willingness to learn and apply knowledge gained through academic studies in a practical workplace environment.
 - Ability to work collaboratively within a team while managing competing priorities and meeting deadlines.
