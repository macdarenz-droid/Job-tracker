This role will involve supporting the delivery of road and bridge infrastructure projects, with a focus on maintenance, rehabilitation and road safety improvements. Working alongside senior engineering staff, you’ll gain exposure to project management, contract administration, construction inspections and technical problem-solving across the project lifecycle.
 Key Responsibilities
 - Assist with managing road infrastructure projects across design, construction and maintenance phases.
 - Administer contracts and support the selection of appropriate procurement and delivery methods.
 - Coordinate road and bridge maintenance activities, including developing suitable maintenance procedures and work instructions.
 - Calculate material quantities, review testing results, identify non-conformances and undertake project risk assessments.
 - Provide technical input into planning and design processes to achieve quality outcomes and value for money.
 - Identify design or construction issues and assist in developing practical solutions.
 - Conduct site inspections, monitor construction quality and manage relevant inspection hold points.
 - Ensure project activities comply with quality management procedures and relevant requirements.
 - Liaise with internal teams, contractors, consultants and other stakeholders to support effective project delivery.
 Successful candidate & requirements 
 - A recognized four-year Bachelor of Engineering degree accredited under the Washington Accord, or an accepted equivalent.
 - Experience or knowledge in civil engineering, ideally involving road infrastructure, bridge maintenance, construction or asset maintenance.
 - An understanding of project management, contract administration and construction processes.
 - Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to assess technical information and identify practical solutions.
 - Good communication and stakeholder management skills.
 - A proactive approach, with the ability to work collaboratively while managing tasks and priorities.
 - A strong focus on quality, safety, risk management and continuous improvement.
 - Current C-class driver’s license.
 - Construction Industry Induction (White Card).
 - Suitable personal protective equipment (PPE).
 - Ability to complete criminal history screening.
