Are you ready to turn your degree into something extraordinary?
At Sitzler, you'll have the opportunity to work on some of Australia's most exciting, and complex commercial construction projects while developing the skills, confidence, and experience needed for a successful long-term career in the construction industry.
Our two-year Graduate Program has been designed for recent graduates who are ready to step out of the classroom and into the real world of construction. Through three structured rotations, you'll gain exposure to different aspects of the business, work alongside experienced industry professionals and discover where your strengths and interests lie. 
This is not a role where you'll spend your days watching from the sidelines. You'll be involved in real projects, real challenges and real career-building opportunities from day one.
Who We're Looking For
We're seeking graduates who are excited about building a career in construction and ready to gain hands-on experience.
To be eligible, you must:
 - Have completed a relevant degree within the last two years in: 
 - Construction Management
 - Engineering (Civil, Structural, Mechanical, etc.)
 - Project Management
 - Estimating or Quantity Surveying
 - Hold Australian Citizenship or Permanent Residency
 - Have a current driver's licence
 - Be willing to work on project sites and travel where required
Skills & Attributes We're Looking For
The successful candidates will demonstrate:
 - A proactive approach and willingness to take ownership of tasks
 - Strong communication and interpersonal skills
 - Effective organisation and time management skills
 - The ability to work both independently and collaboratively within a team
 - Adaptability and a positive attitude when faced with new challenges
 - A commitment to delivering high-quality work and continuous improvement
Why Join Sitzler?
You'll work on major construction projects, learn from experienced industry leaders, rotate through different areas of the business, and gain practical experience. Most importantly, you'll be joining a company that is committed to developing the next generation of construction professionals.
Supporting Our People
At Sitzler, we believe in rewarding our people with benefits that support their careers, families, and wellbeing, including:
 - Paid Parental Leave – Supporting employees through important family milestones with paid parental leave entitlements.
 - Employee Referral Program – Earn rewards for helping us attract great people by referring successful candidates to join our team.
 - Company-provided Christmas Shutdown – Enjoy a well-earned break over the festive season with a company-provided week of leave during our annual Christmas shutdown, allowing you to recharge without using your annual leave balance.
