Site / Project Engineer - Civil & Concrete
Oakleigh Civil - South Australia
Join a growing South Australian civil construction company and build your career across project engineering, construction delivery, estimating and commercial management. 
About Oakleigh Civil 
Oakleigh Civil is a South Australian civil construction company specialising in civil infrastructure and structural concrete projects. Our capabilities include formwork, reinforcement, concrete placement and excavation. 
We work with on major infrastructure projects across metropolitan and regional South Australia, as well as interstate. 
Our work spans water infrastructure, defence, renewable energy, roads, mining and electrical infrastructure. 
Our experienced team takes a practical approach to delivering projects safely and to a very high standard. We value strong client relationships and take pride in being a contractor our clients can rely on.
About the role 
We are seeking a motivated Site / Project Engineer with at least three years’ construction experience to join our growing team. 
Working closely with the Construction Manager, company management, site supervisors and clients, you will help plan, coordinate and deliver projects from tender stage through to completion.  
The role combines site-based project management, estimating and commercial responsibilities. Unlike a traditional site engineering role, you will have exposure to the full project lifecycle, including estimating, procurement, construction delivery, cost control, variations, progress claims and project close-out. 
The successful candidate will be expected to take ownership of assigned projects, identify issues early and work with the project team to develop practical solutions. 
This position suits someone who is looking to broaden their capabilities, take greater responsibility and develop their career within a growing civil construction business.
Key responsibilities 
- Plan and coordinate construction activities with site supervisors to meet project requirements and construction programs. 
- Take ownership of allocated projects from planning through to completion. 
- Maintain regular communication with clients regarding project progress, upcoming works and any issues requiring resolution. 
- Review drawings, specifications and scope documents, identify issues, discrepancies, risks and constructability issues. 
- Assist with estimating, quantity take-offs, quotations and tender submissions. 
- Prepare and maintain project schedules, budgets, forecasts and progress reports. 
- Monitor project costs, cost-to-complete and forecasts. 
- Prepare and track project costs, variations and progress claims. 
- Coordinate procurement of materials, subcontractors and suppliers in accordance with project requirements and programs. 
- Coordinate project quality requirements, including ITP’s, inspections, testing, NCR’s and handover documentation. 
- Maintain accurate project documentation, registers, correspondence and records. 
- Support site safety requirements and work with supervisors to ensure works are planned and delivered safely. 
- Provide practical engineering and constructability advice to site teams. 
- Build and maintain effective relationships with clients, contractors and suppliers.

- A practical approach, initiative and willingness to work closely with site supervisors and site crews.

- A genuine desire to develop your skills and progress your career within a growing construction business.
