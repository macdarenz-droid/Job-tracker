About Kapitol

The Opportunity

We're now looking for a Digital Engineering Coordinator to join our growing Digital Engineering team.

Working across complex commercial construction projects, you'll help bring together design, technology and construction by coordinating multidisciplinary models, identifying and resolving issues before they reach site, and ensuring digital delivery supports successful project outcomes from design through to handover.

This is a highly collaborative role where you'll work closely with Design Managers, Project Teams, Consultants and Trade Partners, driving coordination outcomes, constructability reviews, information management and model-based delivery across the project lifecycle.

You'll have the opportunity to work with industry-leading technology including while contributing to the continued growth and maturity of Digital Engineering at Kapitol.

Key Responsibilities;

Coordinate, federate and audit multidisciplinary BIM models across consultants, trade partners and project teams.
Run clash detection and constructability reviews, identifying and resolving issues before they impact site delivery.
Facilitate model coordination meetings and drive timely closure of design and coordination actions.
Support the implementation and governance of project Digital Engineering Management Plans (DEMPs).
Manage project Common Data Environments (CDEs), including Autodesk Forma and Aconex.
Conduct model audits and report on model quality, coordination performance and project risks.
Utilise reality capture technology to validate site conditions, installations and as-built outcomes.
Support construction sequencing, logistics planning and model-based project delivery.
Deliver site-verified as-built models and structured asset information for client handover and facilities management requirements.
Provide technical support, mentoring and guidance to project stakeholders while championing Digital Engineering best practice.
Contribute to continuous improvement initiatives, automation opportunities and capability development across the business.
