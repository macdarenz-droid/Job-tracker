Business Unit

The Enterprise Project Management Office supports the effective planning, governance and delivery of Council's project portfolio. The team provides consistent project management frameworks, portfolio reporting and oversight to support informed decision-making and the successful delivery of Council projects.

The Opportunity

As the Project Program Officer, you will play an important role in bringing together scheduling, financial and project information to provide a clear picture of Council's project portfolio. You will maintain project and portfolio schedules, bring together budget, schedule and commitment information, and develop reporting packs and dashboards that support effective project oversight. Working across the Enterprise Project Management Office, you will collaborate with Project Leads, Finance and commercial staff to ensure reporting is accurate, consistent and up to date.

This is a full-time, ongoing position based from Council's Bruce Road Depot in Kalimna West and operates under Council's hybrid working model. An initial period of full-time office-based work will be required to support onboarding and training, with hybrid working arrangements available once the successful candidate has developed sufficient knowledge and capability in the role. While hybrid work flexibility is available, this role cannot be completed living outside of the region - an ongoing expectation of working from the office at least three days per week applies.

Key Responsibilities Include

Build and maintain Council's master portfolio schedule and individual project schedules.
Bring together schedule, budget and financial commitment information to support accurate forecasting and reporting.
Develop and maintain monthly portfolio reporting packs and dashboards using Excel and Power BI.
Identify and analyse differences between project schedules, budgets and financial commitments.
Coordinate reporting information across project, commercial and finance teams to support monthly and quarterly reporting.


What We're Looking For

Experience in project scheduling, portfolio reporting or a project/program office environment.
Strong scheduling skills, including experience managing baselines and project progress using MS Project or similar software.
Advanced Excel skills and demonstrated experience building and maintaining Power BI dashboards.
An understanding of how project schedules, budgets and financial commitments work together.
A qualification in project management, business or a related field, or equivalent experience, along with a current Australian driver's licence.
