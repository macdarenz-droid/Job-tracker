As a Technical Officer, you will work with an energetic team of research, technical and extension officers in the Farming Systems Innovation team across Queensland, joining the rapidly increasing team based in Cloncurry, to assess new farming systems and practices on farms and help growers and agronomists to implement the best of these practices on their farms.

The Opportunity

As Our Technical Officer, You Will

 Provide technical support to the Senior Research Agronomist in Mareeba, and Research Agronomists in Cloncurry and actively participate in the planning, organising and conduct RD&E to deliver the “Unlocking Farming Systems for North Queensland Initiative” which is a strategic partnership between CRDC, GRDC and DPI.
 Assume responsibility for the planning and conduct project activities that drive productive, profitable, and resilient cropping farming systems in Northern Queensland (NQ). Specific duties will include: monitoring crops and implementing field treatments that include fertilisers, herbicides and insecticides in accordance with best management practice, and collecting and preparing soil, plant, and water samples including plant biomass, grain, soil, and other samples for analysis from field trials to characterise aspects of crop performance, soil health and farming system sustainability.
 Work in a team and collaborate with and support research agronomists and farmers to co-ordinate and conduct research in local farming systems.
 Liaise and co-ordinate project activities with commercial, departmental and interstate co-operators.
 Undertake and supervise field operations to support the RD&E program, including the use of mechanical equipment for the planting, growing, and harvesting operations in remote environments.
 Actively participate as a member of a multidisciplinary team, building effective working relationships.
 Supervise operational staff including field operation activities (see above) and the collection of samples and field data.
 Check and summarise experimental data in preparation for statistical analysis and communicate them to the responsible scientist.
 Assist in the compilation of material for project reports, extension publications and research papers for departmental, commercial clients and funding bodies.
 Participate in local planning within the Farming Systems Innovation Team and liaise with DPI district/regional agronomists, technical staff, research station staff and farmer co-operators to support other research projects in across Queensland.



Technical Requirements Of The Role

 Knowledge of, of emerging issues in modern farming systems in Northern Queensland and challenges for profitability and sustainability of natural resources in the region.
 Practical agronomic knowledge of grain and cotton production systems, including the collection and management of agronomic, soil and crop performance data.
 Well-developed stakeholder engagement and communication skills.
 Demonstrated ability to safely operate farm equipment.



What We Are Looking For (qualifications, Registrations, Experience)

There are some qualifications that we see as essential. You must possess a:

 Diploma in Applied Science, Rural Science, or Rural Techniques from a recognised tertiary institution, or equivalent qualifications in a field relevant to the position.
 Current ‘C' class drivers licence (preferably manual), and desirably hold a truck licence
