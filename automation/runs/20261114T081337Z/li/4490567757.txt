Design the future of a growing city.

Help shape how Blacktown City manages the parks, playgrounds, sportsfields and recreation assets our community uses every day.

As Asset Engineer, you manage asset information and turn condition, risk, utilisation and service data into practical maintenance, renewal and capital programs. You validate asset registers, coordinate condition and compliance inspections, maintain the Open Space Asset Management Plan and develop a 20-year renewal forecast. You also identify renewal backlogs and prioritise projects based on risk, utilisation and service needs.

Within Asset Planning and Support, you prepare annual renewal and long-term financial plan inputs, develop lifecycle strategies for playgrounds, sportsfields and park structures, and support renewal scopes and asset handovers. You also contribute to works programs, tender assessments, grants, contribution plans, works-in-kind agreements and statutory reporting.

Your work helps Council understand when assets need attention and where investment has the greatest value for the community.
