WSP’s National Technical Contracting – Geotechnical Laboratories team plays a critical role supporting our Earth & Environment and Geotechnical & Tunnels businesses across Australia. Due to continued growth, we are seeking Trainee, Junior and Intermediate Geotechnical Laboratory Technicians to join our expanding NATA-accredited laboratory network.

As a Trainee Laboratory Technician You'll

Safely retrieve, prepare and test samples including soil, rock, aggregate and concrete
Undertake routine laboratory testing under supervision in line with relevant standards and ISO 17025 requirements
Operate and assist with maintenance and calibration of laboratory equipment
Support logistics including sample handling, storage, shipping and disposal
Work collaboratively with engineers, scientists and laboratory teams to meet project deadlines
Participate in site visits and project‑specific rosters as required
Maintain accurate records including timesheets and expenses
Actively contribute to a strong health, safety and environmental culture


What You'll Bring

Strong attention to detail with good time management skills
Clear written and verbal communication skills
Basic mathematics and working knowledge of Microsoft Office (especially Excel)
Enthusiastic, organised and keen to learn
Completed high school mathematics (essential)
Certificate III in Laboratory Techniques is advantageous but not required
Ability to obtain a White Card and undertake first aid training
Hold a valid Australian driver’s license
