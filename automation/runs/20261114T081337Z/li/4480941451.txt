You’re an experienced civil engineer looking to take the next step in your career. You will lead project teams, taking ownership of the technical delivery of civil projects across built form, road infrastructure, high-density precinct development, and public domain works.

This role is based at our Newcastle office.

What You’ll Own

As a Civil Engineer, you will be joining a professional and collaborative team of engineers, designers, drafters, graduates and undergraduates, working on a diverse range of projects across the built environment.

With a strong pipeline of upcoming projects for both new and existing clients, you will lead projects while being supported by a close-knit management team. You will be client-facing, with the autonomy of leading projects from concept through to construction and technical delivery, while having the support you need to succeed. You will act as a mentor to emerging engineers contributing to their technical development and continued growth of the civil section.

You will be part of a growing office with a great culture, including regular team catch-ups, office events and social activities.

Why Northrop

We’re employee owned. That means the people working in our business have a genuine stake in its future.

There are no external shareholders or overseas parent company. Decisions are made by people who understand our work, our clients and our people. Our leaders are accessible, and when you have an idea for doing something better, you’ll be encouraged to share it.

For more than 50 years, we’ve built our business around our people, our relationships and the impact our work has on communities.

Today, our multidisciplinary teams work across NSW, ACT, Queensland and Victoria on projects of all sizes, spanning sectors including health, education, residential, commercial, industrial, sport and land development.

Essential

What You Bring

Ideally 3-5 Years of post-graduate experience working as a civil engineering consultant on built form projects.
Bachelor's degree in Engineering from a recognised university or institute.
Experience preparing detailed design and documentation of roads, intersections, pavements, earthworks and stormwater drainage.
The ability to manage multiple tasks at the same time, whilst working autonomously.
Sound understanding of civil design software such as 12D Model and/or Civil 3D.
Competent and independent use of other civil modelling software such as DRAINS, MUSIC and AutoCAD.
