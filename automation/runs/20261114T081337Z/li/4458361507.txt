Junior Supervisor - Project Homes - Opportunities in NW & SW Sydney

The Role

This is a rare opportunity for a qualified Carpenter to transition into a Junior Site Supervisor role with the full backing of a highly experienced Construction Manager and an in-house Training & Development Manager.

You’ll be mentored every step of the way as you learn to:

 Manage daily site operations and construction workflows 
 Coordinate trades, suppliers, and materials to meet build schedules 
 Conduct site inspections to ensure quality and compliance with BCA/NCC and Australian Standards 
 Maintain regular, professional communication with clients throughout the build 
 Work closely with estimating, admin, trade allocation, and maintenance teams to keep projects moving smoothly 
 Uphold company safety standards and site presentation at all times 
 Salary: $85k - $110k + Super + Car + Fuel Car 


About You

You’ll bring the technical skills of your Carpentry trade background along with the drive to step up into supervision. Specifically, you’ll have:

 Cert III in Carpentry - essential 
 Cert IV in Building & Construction - essential 
 Proven experience in Frames & Trusses for a project home builder - essential 
 Experience dealing with customers directly 
 Strong communication skills - articulate, professional, and approachable 
 A proactive, motivated mindset with good tenure in previous roles 
 The ability to build rapport with trades, clients, and colleagues 


Why Join

If you’re ready to take the next step in your construction career, this is a unique opportunity to learn directly from some of the industry’s best. You’ll join a respected, award-winning builder with a reputation for looking after their people.
