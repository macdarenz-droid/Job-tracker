About the role
We are seeking a Project Officer who will manage and support the delivery of assigned projects in Council’s Capital Works Program which could include a variety of projects from playgrounds, sports fields, buildings, asset renewals, engineering and infrastructure projects.
Candidates must be from a construction or civil/ structural engineering background.


Key Responsibilities:
Coordinate and prioritise key tasks and activities, anticipate project constraints, identify inter-dependencies, and manage these to deliver an optimum outcome for Council.
Develop achievable project plans for the effective implementation and management of procurement, time, communications, change, risks, issues and budgets/ costs, tailored to support and align with Council’s operations.
Manage the project budget and proactively work with Finance to ensure asset management data is captured for the long-term sustainability of Council assets.
Deliver timely outputs and produce or support the development of the required reports professionally and accurately.
Perform prudent and proactive contract administration to safeguard and protect Council’s interests and mitigate risk exposure.
Develop stakeholder engagement and communication plans and working with other council officers across the different business units, proactively manage stakeholder expectations.
Coordinate and communicate with internal and external stakeholders, ensuring clear understanding of project goals and progress.
Work across Council to ensure the appropriate internal teams/resources are engaged in the development and delivery of the project
Ensure project deliverables meet quality standards and align with established best practices.


About the Person
To be successful in this position, you will have:
Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Project or Construction Management or similar.
Australian Institute of Project Management (AIPM) certification or equivalent advantageous.
Some demonstrable experience in successfully managing and delivering projects within a government or public sector context.
Strong understanding of local government operations, regulations, and decision-making processes.
Proficiency in project management tools and software such as MS Project
Current Class “C” Drivers Licence
Workcover accredited Construction Induction (White) Card preferred
