What’s the focus of this position? 
The Graduate position provides recent graduates with the opportunity to develop professional skills and experience across City of Newcastle while contributing to meaningful projects and operational activities. Graduates rotate through different service units and teams to gain exposure to a variety of functions, build organisational knowledge, and develop capabilities that support future career growth. 

The Graduate Asset Engineer position will apply their theoretical knowledge to provide engineering advice that will inform and support the development and delivery of CN’s capital works program and programmed maintenance activities. This position will undertake investigations and inspections and utilise your knowledge to assess and recommend solutions on infrastructure matters, including the development of business cases, project briefs, project scoping documents, review of completed works and compiling quality and contract documentation. 
Working in the City Infrastructure directorate, you will gain experience in the cradle to grave delivery of infrastructure projects across a variety of asset classes including roads, drainage and structures. This will include the coordination of both internal and external resources during delivery. You will perform functions across the Asset Management, Project Planning and delivery spaces, whilst continuing to learn, ultimately working towards establishing an ongoing engineering career at CN.
The purpose of this role is to develop professional work skills within a discipline related business unit and contribute to business outcomes through research, analysis, project support, stakeholder engagement, and continuous improvement activities while applying academic knowledge in a practical workplace environment.

What you will be doing:
Assist in the development and delivery of contracts associated with key engineering projects and maintenance activities with appropriate consideration to Quality, Safety, Environmental and Traffic management systems.
Prepare reports and appropriate recommendations to key stakeholders across the business. 
Review technical drawings and documents in relation to CN’s standards and policies. 
Assist in the coordination and delivery of Civil Projects and Maintenance activities. 
Assist in the provision of contract and tender documentation.
Provide specialist technical support and advice to internal and external customers.
Undertake field-based work including construction, surveillance, investigations and inspections as required.
Carry out work in a manner that meets the expectations of CN’s CREW ideals of Collaboration, Respect, Excellence and Wellbeing.
Any other accountabilities or duties as directed by the Manager which are within the employee’s skill, competence, and training.

The essentials you’ll need:
Bachelor of Engineering (Civil, Structural, Environmental) and eligibility for admission into the Institution of Engineers Australia.
Knowledge and understanding of Work Health and Safety (WHS) management practices, processes and requirements.
Strong written, verbal, and interpersonal communication skills. 
Ability to think analytically to solve problems and convert theoretical knowledge into practical solutions.  
Demonstrated customer service and teamwork skills with the ability to build effective working relationships across CN and with external service providers as required.
Excellent organisational skills with the ability to apply a high level of knowledge and skills to achieve results in line with department goals. 
Strong computer skills, including Microsoft Office (Word and Excel).
Willingness to work at any location within Newcastle’s local government area, both office and field based, subject to organisational requirements. 
Possession of a current "C" Class Driver's Licence. 
Possession of a White Card. 

Other valuable skills you may have:

Asset management, construction and/or project management experience.
